# Sythis, Harvest's Hand — Pilot Primer ("Green White Cushion Castle 25")

## 1. Identity and game plan

- **Commander:** Sythis, Harvest's Hand — {G}{W} 1/1 Legendary Enchantment Creature, *"Whenever you cast an enchantment spell, you gain 1 life and draw a card."* She is a draw engine that lives in the command zone, not a combo piece. Every cheap enchantment is a cantrip (or a 2–4 card draw with more enchantresses out), and every cast is +1 life toward Test of Endurance.
- **Archetype (EDHREC: Enchantress ≫ Auras > Lifegain > Combo; ~14k decks):** this build is *Enchantress value → fort ("cushion castle") → two-card infinite-mana/token combo → deterministic kill*, with real subthemes that dictate sequencing:
  - **Draw web:** Sythis, Argothian Enchantress, Enchantress's Presence (cast triggers); Eidolon of Blossoms (constellation ETB); Kenrith's Transformation, Composer of Spring.
  - **Big mana:** Sanctum Weaver, Serra's Sanctum, Gaea's Cradle, Nykthos, Nyxbloom Ancient, Mirari's Wake, land auras (Utopia Sprawl, Wild Growth, Fertile Ground, Overgrowth, Wolfwillow Haven), Earthcraft, Enduring Vitality, Smothering Tithe, Sol Ring/Thought Vessel.
  - **Copy/ETB package:** Ondu Spiritdancer, Springheart Nantuko, Weaver of Harmony, Elesh Norn, Secret Arcade.
  - **Fort:** Sphere of Safety, Solitary Confinement, Blind Obedience, Curse of Exhaustion, Arasta, Elesh Norn, Aura Shards.
  - **Lifegain → Test of Endurance:** Sythis, Herald of the Pantheon, Daxos, Courser, Blind Obedience extort, lifelinkers (Jukai, Alseid, Katilda), Luminarch-style stall.
  - **Aura Voltron (backup):** Shield of the Oversoul, Strength of the Harvest, Tempest Technique, Nyxborn Hydra (bestow), Katilda's Rising Dawn, Dusty Parlor counters.
- **Deck skeleton:** 49 nonland enchantment cards + Urza's Saga and Valgavoth's Lair (enchantment lands) + Katilda's disturb face; 19 non-enchantment spells; 30 lands (only **4 Forest + 4 Plains are basic** — this matters for Earthcraft and Utopia Sprawl).
- **How strong pilots play it:** 1-mana land aura on turn 1, Sythis turn 2, a second enchantress and/or cost reducer turn 3, then chain cheap enchantments every turn so each one draws 2–4. Sanctum Weaver is the best card in the deck: it is both the ramp spell and half of the main combo. Fort (Sphere/Confinement) goes down only when the table turns hostile; stay politically quiet, strip opposing engines with Grasp/Banishing/Quarantine, tutor the missing combo half, and win inside protection (Heroic Intervention / Teferi's Protection / Destiny Spinner making the pieces uncounterable). There are **no counterspells** in this deck: you win through timing, redundancy and protection, not by answering.
- **Clock:** fair plan kills around turns 9–12; combo with a tutor turns 5–8. Library size is the hard cap on every loop that involves Sythis/Eidolon draws — all enchantress draws are **mandatory**.

## 3. Engines and synergy packages (these decide your sequencing)

### 3.1 Draw engine
- **Cast** triggers (Sythis, Argothian, Presence) fire on casting: a countered enchantment still draws; tokens/copies (Ondu tokens, Tempest Technique storm copies) are not cast → no cast draw. **ETB** trigger (Eidolon) fires on entering: tokens, Replenish returns, Saga/Lair entering, Ondu copies all draw. Every one of these is mandatory — decide loop counts with the library in mind.
- Within a turn: **enchantress / reducer first, cheap enchantments after** (every later spell draws more). Presence before Wild Growth, never after. Cast the 1-drops last only if they are the ones you can afford to lose to a counter.
- **Tutor-to-top → Sythis draw:** Enlightened Tutor, Sterling Grove, Moon-Blessed Cleric and Hall of Heliod's Generosity put a card on top; casting any enchantment afterwards draws it the same turn. Enlightened Tutor in your precombat main followed by a 1-mana aura = "Idyllic Tutor for {W}+{1}".
- **Secret Arcade** (the {4}{W} door) makes all your nonland permanents and permanent spells enchantments: creatures, Sol Ring, Thought Vessel now draw off Sythis/Argothian/Presence, get the cost reducers, are uncounterable under Destiny Spinner, get Dusty Parlor counters, and count for Sanctum Weaver, Serra's Sanctum, Sphere of Safety, Katilda, Strength of the Harvest and Destiny Spinner. Arcade + Eidolon: every permanent that enters (Angel tokens, Treasures, Ondu copies, Constructs) is a mandatory draw — track the library.
- **Redress Fate** miracle: if it is the first card you draw in a turn (natural draw step, or the first Sythis draw on a turn where Solitary Confinement skipped your draw step), cast it for {3}{W} → all artifact and enchantment cards from your graveyard return. Cost reducers don't apply (sorcery).
- Reliquary Tower / Thought Vessel: no discards to hand size; keep one spare card for Solitary Confinement's upkeep.

### 3.2 Mana engine
- Land auras go on **untapped** lands and the land is tapped immediately. Utopia Sprawl needs a Forest: basics, Temple Garden, Savannah, Canopy Vista, Lush Portico, or any land while Yavimaya / Dryad of the Ilysian Grove is out (if that effect leaves, a Sprawl on a non-Forest is put into the graveyard — prefer real Forests; a **basic** Forest naming White if Earthcraft/Luminarch is a live plan).
- **Sanctum Weaver** ({T}: X mana of one color, X = enchantments you control) is summoning-sick the turn it enters unless Concordant Crossroads is out. From 6+ enchantments it pays for an entire turn by itself; X is counted on resolution, so cast enchantments before tapping it.
- Serra's Sanctum (W per enchantment — Auras, enchantment creatures, Saga, Lair, Sythis all count), Gaea's Cradle (G per creature, tokens and Spinner-animated lands included), Nykthos (devotion; hybrid {G/W} symbols count for both colors). **Nyxbloom Ancient** triples mana from any permanent you *tap* (lands, Sol Ring → {C}{C}{C}{C}{C}{C}, Weaver → 3X, Sanctum, Cradle, Nykthos, Treasures, Enduring Vitality creatures) — the extra mana from land auras / Mirari's Wake triggers is not tripled (it isn't produced by the tapped permanent).
- **Earthcraft as fair ramp:** its cost is "tap an untapped creature you control" — summoning-sick creatures and fresh tokens can pay it (it is Earthcraft's ability, not the creature's {T} ability; 302.6 doesn't apply). Each creature = one extra untap of an aura'd basic per turn; Overgrowth on a basic Plains = +3 mana per creature tapped. It only untaps **basic** lands.
- Cost reducers cut generic in any enchantment spell: X spells keep X (Nyxborn Hydra X=4 costs {4}{G} → {1}{G} with three reducers and still enters with 4 counters; Quarantine Field X=2 = {4}{W}{W} → {1}{W}{W}), disturb ({3}{W}{W} → {W}{W}), bestow, Sphere of Safety ({1}{W}). They do **not** reduce Room unlock costs or activated abilities.
- Enduring Vitality: your creatures tap for any color (unsick ones); it comes back as a noncreature enchantment when it dies and keeps granting.
- Composer of Spring: every enchantment ETB → a land from hand tapped; with 6+ enchantments → a **creature or land** from hand tapped (Nyxbloom, Elesh Norn, Ondu, Eidolon for free — but "put" is not "cast": no Sythis draw, no reducers needed). Hold lands in hand to feed it. Courser plays lands off the top; Dryad gives the extra drop; Shigeki digs a land and bins 3 cards (delirium fuel for Shifting Woodland, Replenish fuel).
- Smothering Tithe: Treasures (×3 under Nyxbloom). Blind Obedience makes *opponents'* Treasures enter tapped.

### 3.3 Copy / ETB package
- **Ondu Spiritdancer** (3/3, not an enchantment itself unless Arcade): whenever an enchantment you control enters, you may make a token copy — **each Ondu object once per turn** (a token copy of Ondu is a new object with its own "once"). Token Auras enter attached to a legal object you choose (no targeting — shroud doesn't stop it; a Sprawl copy chooses a color as it enters). Best copies without Arcade: Nyxbloom Ancient (×9), Sanctum Weaver, Grasp of Fate (token ETB exiles again), Banishing Light, Eidolon / Presence, Sphere / Smothering Tithe / Mirari's Wake, Aura Shards, Gauntlets of Light (put it on Weaver of Harmony or a second Weaver), Song of the Dryads (onto another opponent's commander), Kenrith's Transformation, Utopia Sprawl/Overgrowth, even Urza's Saga or Valgavoth's Lair entering (a free land). Worthless copies — decline: Luminarch (0 counters), Quarantine Field (enters with X=0 counters), any legendary (legend rule), Secret Arcade/Dusty Parlor (a copied Room enters with both doors **locked**). With several Ondus out, each may copy a *different* enchantment that turn — save the "may" for the best one.
- **Springheart Nantuko** bestowed ({1}{G}) on a creature: each landfall, pay {1}{G} → token copy of that creature (otherwise a 1/1 Insect). Hosts: Sanctum Weaver, Eidolon, Ondu, Nyxbloom, Arasta(no—legendary), never Sythis/Katilda/Shigeki (legend rule), never Argothian (shroud) or anything under Sterling Grove's shroud. Landfalls per turn: Composer, Dryad, Courser, Windswept Heath (2), Shigeki, MDFC lands. Elesh Norn doubles it.
- **Weaver of Harmony** (2/2; {G},{T}, needs to be unsick): copy an activated or triggered ability of an enchantment source you control. Mana abilities can't be targeted (Sanctum Weaver's tap, Serra's Sanctum, land-aura triggers are out). Best targets: Sythis's cast trigger (+1 card, +1 life), Grasp / Banishing / Quarantine Field ETB (more exiles; the QF copy uses the counters on the Field), Luminarch's Angel activation (two Angels), Luminarch's end-step counter trigger (+1 counter), Meticulous Excavation activation (bounce two), Destiny Spinner activation (animate two lands), Composer, Springheart landfall, Kenrith's draw, Wolfwillow's sac ability (the copy makes a Wolf with no sacrifice), Blind Obedience's extort trigger (pay {W} again → another drain), Eidolon's constellation, Alseid's ability, Urza's Saga chapter II, Smothering Tithe's trigger (a second Treasure unless they pay twice), Gift of Immortality's return. Also an anthem for your enchantment creatures (+1/+1).
- **Elesh Norn, Mother of Machines:** every trigger of yours caused by a permanent entering fires twice — Eidolon, Composer (two drops), Daxos, Aura Shards, Grasp / Banishing / Quarantine Field ETB, Kenrith's draw, Springheart and Courser landfall, Moon-Blessed Cleric (second search shuffles the first pick; net one card on top). Ondu's trigger doubles but "once each turn" wastes the second. Opponents' ETB triggers are switched off entirely (Thassa's Oracle, Dockside, Gray Merchant, landfall, constellation, their Eternal Witness…). She is a creature, not an enchantment, unless Arcade is out.
- **Dusty Parlor** (the {2}{W} door): every enchantment spell you cast puts its MV in +1/+1 counters on up to one target creature — Sythis with Shield of the Oversoul is the usual target; a Quarantine Field X=3 is MV 8; Katilda's disturb spell is MV 3; Hydra bestowed X=5 is MV 6.

### 3.4 Fort, protection, and the Sterling Grove trap
- **Sphere of Safety:** X mana per attacking creature, X = your enchantments — irrelevant under 5, decisive at 10+. **Solitary Confinement:** skip your draw step (Sythis draws instead), discard at each upkeep or sacrifice it, you have shroud, all damage to you is prevented (commander damage included; life loss/drain is not). **Blind Obedience:** opposing creatures and artifacts (rocks, Treasures) enter tapped — this also blanks the haste Concordant Crossroads hands out; extort on every spell you cast. **Curse of Exhaustion:** on the storm / combo / blue player — they get one spell per turn, *including on your turn*. **Arasta:** 1/2 reach Spider per opposing instant/sorcery (Earthcraft fodder, Cradle mana, convoke). **Elesh Norn:** no opposing ETB triggers.
- **Hexproof vs shroud:** Privileged Position (other permanents hexproof — lands included, so Sanctum/Cradle are safe from Strip Mine effects) and Asceticism (creatures hexproof, {1}{G} regenerate) still let *you* target your stuff. **Sterling Grove gives your other enchantments shroud — including your enchantment creatures** (Sythis, Weaver, Eidolon, Daxos, Spinner, Jukai…): while Grove is out you cannot cast Gauntlets of Light, Shield of the Oversoul, Gift of Immortality, Timely Ward, Katilda's Rising Dawn, Strength of the Harvest, Tempest Technique, bestow Hydra/Springheart/Trickster's Elk onto them, nor target them with Meticulous Excavation, Alseid or Asceticism's regeneration. Rule: **Auras first, Grove after**, or sac Grove ({1}, tutor to top) first. Under Secret Arcade, Grove shrouds *every* nonland permanent you control.
- **Instant-speed saves:** Heroic Intervention ({1}{G}: hexproof+indestructible — doesn't stop exile/bounce/sacrifice wipes), Teferi's Protection ({2}{W}: everything phases out, life locked until your next turn), Clever Concealment ({2}{W}{W} convoke — tap summoning-sick creatures and tokens to pay; phases out nonland permanents with their Auras), Timely Ward (flash only when targeting Sythis), Alseid ({1}, sac: protection from one color to a creature or enchantment — note protection from white/green knocks your own Auras of that color off the recipient), Meticulous Excavation ({2}{W}, your turn only: bounce the targeted piece; a bounced Sythis recasts from hand for {G}{W} with no tax), Asceticism regeneration, Eiganjo channel (4 damage to an attacker/blocker; costs {1} less per legendary creature you control).
- **Rebuild after a wrath:** Replenish / Redress Fate return everything; returning Auras attach to objects you choose (no targeting, so shroud is irrelevant); Secret Arcade comes back with both doors locked ({2}{W}/{4}{W} to unlock again, as a sorcery); Urza's Saga and Valgavoth's Lair come back (they are enchantment cards); Nyxborn Hydra returns as a 0/0 and dies unless Weaver of Harmony / Mirari's Wake / an Aura raises it; Katilda's Rising Dawn is exiled instead of going to a graveyard; Eidolon draws once per returning enchantment (mandatory), Ondu copies one, Composer drops a land/creature per ETB.
- **Gift of Immortality on Sythis:** when she dies, *leave her in the graveyard* (decline the command-zone replacement) — Gift returns her to the battlefield immediately and re-attaches at the next end step; she re-enters → Eidolon draw, Composer, Daxos, Aura Shards trigger. On Sanctum Weaver or Eidolon it is equally good.

### 3.5 Lifegain → Test of Endurance
- Per turn: Sythis +1 and Herald +1 per enchantment cast; extort {W} per spell = +3 (and −1 to each opponent); Daxos +1 per creature ETB or death; Courser +1 per land; Katilda / Jukai / Alseid / Katilda's Rising Dawn lifelink; Weaver of Harmony copying Sythis. A normal enchantress turn is +4 to +10 life. 40 → 50 is two such turns.
- **Test of Endurance** wins at the beginning of your upkeep if you have 50+ life. Once that trigger is on the stack, destroying Test does not stop it — only dropping your life below 50 before it resolves (or countering the ability) does. Deployment patterns, best first:
  1. **Emergence Zone** ({1},{T}, sac): cast Test at the last opponent's end step while at 50+; the table has only instant-speed life-loss/trigger-counter answers.
  2. Cast Test main phase at 50+ and hold **Teferi's Protection** (life can't change, everything phases out, phases back in during your untap → upkeep win) or **Clever Concealment** (phase out Test and the board; convoke).
  3. Cast Test at 45+ with a full enchantress turn ahead (each cast = +1 to +3 life) and Heroic Intervention up.
- Test is an enchantment: Enlightened/Idyllic/Sterling Grove/Cleric find it; casting it is itself +1 life.

### 3.6 Aura Voltron (backup, and the Sythis-hits-for-lethal line)
- Sythis is green **and** white: Shield of the Oversoul gives +2/+2, indestructible **and** flying. Add Katilda's Rising Dawn (+X/+X, flying, lifelink; X = Spirits + enchantments), Strength of the Harvest (+1/+1 per creature/enchantment), Dusty Parlor counters, Nyxborn Hydra bestow (+X, trample, reach), Springheart (+1). 21 commander damage comes fast; with Rising Dawn the lifelink swing is +15–25 life → Test of Endurance.
- **Tempest Technique (storm)** as the finisher of a long chain: after N spells this turn it makes N Aura-token copies (enchant creature you control — each copy may pick a new creature of yours). Each copy is an enchantment and each gives +E/+E where E = your enchantments (and E grows by N). After 6 spells with 15 enchantments, 7 Techniques on Sythis ≈ +150/+150. Needs evasion (Shield flying, Rising Dawn flying, Hydra trample). Eidolon draws per copy (mandatory).
- Katilda, Dawnhart Martyr front face: {1}{W}{W} flying lifelink, P/T = enchantments + Spirits — a 12/12 lifelinker mid-game; when she dies, disturb her ({3}{W}{W}, reducers apply → {W}{W}) onto Sythis.
- Destiny Spinner ({3}{G}): a land becomes an X/X trample **haste** Elemental until end of turn, X = your enchantments **locked in when the ability resolves** — cast your enchantments first, animate after; repeated activations on the same land just re-lock X. Animate lands you did not tap for mana (tapped creatures can't attack).

## 4. Combos — exact lines

All loops below that involve Sythis / Argothian / Presence / Eidolon draws are **bounded by library size**; choose an iteration count before starting and stop with ≥2 cards in library. Mana empties between steps and phases: build the pool in the phase you spend it.

### 4.1 Sanctum Weaver + Gauntlets of Light → unbounded mana (any colors)
- **Needs:** Weaver untapped and unsick (or Concordant Crossroads), Gauntlets attached to Weaver, ≥4 enchantments you control (Weaver + Gauntlets + Sythis + one more — a land aura, Saga, Lair, anything). Net per cycle = X − 3; with Nyxbloom out Weaver taps for 3X so X ≥ 2 suffices.
- **Line:** tap Weaver → add X of one color (mana ability, no stack). Activate Gauntlets' granted "{2}{W}: untap this creature" (uses the stack; opponents can respond — removal on Weaver in response breaks the loop, so have Heroic Intervention / Teferi's up or do it when they are tapped out). Repeat; alternate colors to build the pools you need. Works at instant speed.
- **Assembly cost:** Weaver {1}{G} (earlier turn) + Gauntlets {2}{W} (→ {W} with two reducers). A T4–5 win-setup with a tutor.
- **Outlets, best first (pick the one you actually hold):**
  1. **Extort kill (4.5)** — deterministic, no draws, no combat.
  2. **Destiny Spinner:** {3}{G} per land; animate every untapped land after casting all enchantments; X/X trample haste; attack, splitting attackers by opponents' life (trample beats chump blocks; Sphere/Propaganda effects on their side still cost mana per attacker — pay it with the infinite pool).
  3. **Luminarch Ascension with 4+ counters:** {1}{W} per 4/4 flier, instant speed. With Crossroads swing now; otherwise make N Angels at the **last opponent's end step** (loop works there: Weaver taps, Gauntlets untap), untap, attack with N hasty-enough fliers (under your control since your turn began). N ≈ 40 is plenty.
  4. **Nyxborn Hydra** bestowed on an unsick attacker (Sythis, Katilda, an Angel) for {X}{G}{G}, X = 50+ — one opponent dies per combat (commander damage if on Sythis). Cast as a creature with X huge only if Crossroads is out.
  5. **Quarantine Field** with X = every opposing nonland permanent (sorcery speed, or at flash speed with Emergence Zone) — a one-sided Farewell; win next turn through an empty board with Sphere/Confinement up.
  6. **Draw-the-deck:** Meticulous Excavation bouncing and recasting cheap enchantments (bounded): every recast draws (Sythis + Argothian + Presence), gains (Sythis, Herald, extort), stacks Dusty Parlor counters — end at ≥50 life with Test of Endurance in play and Teferi's Protection in hand.
  7. Nature's Rhythm X = anything (Elesh Norn / Nyxbloom / Ondu), Shigeki channel X = whole graveyard, Asceticism regeneration on everything, Wolfwillow Wolf, Eiganjo channel — filler.
  8. If Ondu has copied Gauntlets onto **Weaver of Harmony**: infinite copies of any trigger/activation ({G} + {2}{W} per copy) — e.g., each Luminarch activation twice, every Aura Shards trigger twice, Sythis trigger twice.

### 4.2 Earthcraft + Luminarch Ascension (4+ counters) + a basic land that makes ≥ {1}{W} per tap → unbounded Angels
- **Enablers on a basic Plains:** Wild Growth (W+G), Fertile Ground (W+any), Wolfwillow Haven (W+G), Overgrowth (W+GG: +1 surplus per loop), Mirari's Wake (WW), Nyxbloom Ancient (WWW: +1 surplus). **On a basic Forest:** Utopia Sprawl naming **White** (G+W), Fertile Ground (G+W). A basic Forest with only Wild Growth/Overgrowth/Nyxbloom makes no {W} and does not work. Earthcraft untaps **basic lands only** (Windswept Heath can fetch one).
- **Line (all instant speed):** tap the basic (≥{1}{W}) → activate Luminarch {1}{W} → 4/4 flying Angel (resolves) → Earthcraft: tap the new Angel (summoning-sick is fine) → untap the basic (resolves) → repeat. Each loop leaves one **tapped** Angel; choose N. No draws → no library cap, unless Secret Arcade + Eidolon (Angels become enchantments → mandatory draws → stop in time).
- **Outlets:** Daxos (+N life → Test at upkeep), Aura Shards ×N (destroy every opposing artifact and enchantment), Gaea's Cradle for N green (→ Spinner lands, Hydra, Nature's Rhythm), Serra's Sanctum under Arcade; with Overgrowth/Nyxbloom the +1 surplus per loop lets you make extra **untapped** Angels (2 loops per free Angel) → with Crossroads attack this turn; otherwise do it at the last opponent's end step and swing next turn with 4N flying power. Fort through the one turn cycle (Sphere, Heroic, Teferi's).
- **Luminarch upkeep:** counters come at each opponent's end step if you didn't lose life *that turn*; pay life (Heath, Temple Garden) only on your own turn. Weaver of Harmony can copy the counter trigger (2 per end step) → 4 counters by the second round. Sphere/Confinement/Blind Obedience make the counters reliable. Ondu copies of Luminarch are useless (0 counters).

### 4.3 Ondu Spiritdancer + Secret Arcade (door unlocked) → unbounded 3/3 enchantment-creature tokens
- **Needs:** Secret Arcade unlocked ({4}{W} cast, reducers apply; or Dusty Parlor {2}{W} now and {4}{W} unlock later as a sorcery — unlock costs are not reduced). Then cast Ondu ({4}{W}; with Arcade out it is an enchantment spell: reducers cut it to {1}{W} and Sythis/enchantresses draw).
- **Line:** Ondu enters as an enchantment → its own trigger → token copy of Ondu → the token's own trigger → another token copy → … each new Ondu object spends its once-per-turn on itself. Every trigger is a "may": choose N and stop. Each token entering also fires Eidolon (mandatory draw — this is the cap), Daxos +1, Aura Shards (may destroy target artifact/enchantment), Composer (land/creature from hand), and grows Sanctum Weaver X, Serra's Sanctum, Gaea's Cradle, Sphere of Safety, Katilda, Strength, Destiny Spinner X.
- **Kill:** with Concordant Crossroads attack with N hasty 3/3s (4/4 with Weaver of Harmony, +1 Mirari's Wake) — N ≈ 40 per opponent through blockers. Without haste: tap Cradle (N green) → Destiny Spinner on every untapped land, or Hydra bestow onto Sythis; or pass with N tokens + Sphere (X is now enormous) + Heroic/Teferi's up. Either way Aura Shards has already stripped every opposing artifact/enchantment.
- **Next turns:** with N Ondus on board, the next enchantment that enters can be copied N times (N Nyxblooms, N Grasps of Fate = 3N exiles, N Sanctum Weavers).

### 4.4 Sanctum Weaver + Meticulous Excavation (+ Concordant Crossroads) → bounded draw/storm/life engine
- **Needs:** your turn (Excavation), Weaver with haste (Crossroads) or this is one-shot, Weaver not shrouded (no Sterling Grove).
- **Loop:** tap Weaver for X → Excavation {2}{W}: return Weaver to hand → recast {1}{G} ({G} with a reducer) → it enters with haste → repeat. Net mana per loop X − 5 (X − 4 with a reducer). Each loop: Sythis +1 card +1 life, Argothian / Presence draws, Eidolon draw (Weaver is an enchantment entering), Herald +1 life, Dusty Parlor +2 counters, Composer land/creature, Daxos +1, **Aura Shards destroy one opposing artifact/enchantment**, extort {W} (−1 each opponent, +3 you), storm +1 for Tempest Technique.
- **Cap:** library ÷ (draws per loop: 1 with Sythis alone, up to 4 with all enchantresses). Uses: dig to Gauntlets (then go unbounded), wipe the opponents' artifacts and enchantments, make Sythis lethal with Parlor counters, reach 50 life, or storm into Tempest Technique. Any cheap enchantment creature can be the bounced piece (Alseid {W}, Jukai {G}{W}, Eidolon for an extra draw) — only Weaver pays for itself.

### 4.5 The extort kill: Blind Obedience + Meticulous Excavation + unbounded mana (+ any cheap non-enchantment permanent)
- **Needs:** 4.1 online, your turn, Blind Obedience, Meticulous Excavation, and a permanent you can bounce and recast cheaply that is **not** an enchantment (so nothing draws): Sol Ring ({1}) is ideal; Thought Vessel, Herald, Starfield Mystic, Composer, Moon-Blessed Cleric (each recast re-tutors to top), Katilda, Elesh Norn, Ondu also work. Argothian can't be targeted. With Secret Arcade out the recast *is* an enchantment spell → Sythis draws each loop → cap = library (still usually enough).
- **Loop:** {2}{W}: bounce Sol Ring → cast Sol Ring {1} → extort triggers → pay {W} → **each opponent loses 1 life, you gain that much**. Repeat until the highest opposing life total is 0 (40 loops from 40). Life loss, not damage: prevention and Solitary-Confinement-style effects on their side don't stop it; only "life can't change" (Teferi's Protection) or hexproof-irrelevant — it isn't targeted. Weaver of Harmony can copy each extort trigger (+1 drain) and an Ondu copy of Blind Obedience doubles every extort.

### 4.6 Backups and conversions
- **Shifting Woodland** (delirium: 4+ card types in graveyard — Saga sacrifices itself as enchantment+land, Heath, tutors, Alseid, Grove sac all help): {2}{G}{G} → copy of a permanent card in your graveyard until end of turn. Copy a dead **Sanctum Weaver** (the land has been under your control all turn, so it can tap immediately) and cast Gauntlets on it → 4.1 from the graveyard; copy Nyxbloom Ancient for a triple-mana turn; copy Eidolon for a draw turn.
- **Springheart Nantuko on Sanctum Weaver / Eidolon / Ondu:** every landfall ({1}{G}) clones the engine; Windswept Heath is two landfalls.
- **Composer at 6+ enchantments:** put Nyxbloom / Elesh Norn / Ondu from hand onto the battlefield tapped off a 1-mana aura.
- **Enduring Vitality + Luminarch/Ondu tokens:** every token taps for a color next turn (immediately with Crossroads).
- **Smothering Tithe + Nyxbloom:** each Treasure is 3 mana.

## 5. Tutors and finding the pieces
- **Idyllic Tutor** ({2}{W}, to hand): Gauntlets (Weaver out) / Weaver (Gauntlets out — Weaver is an enchantment card) / Meticulous Excavation / Earthcraft / Secret Arcade / Sphere / Solitary Confinement / Test of Endurance / Asceticism.
- **Enlightened Tutor** ({W}, instant, top): same targets plus Sol Ring; at an opponent's end step, or precombat main followed by an enchantment cast to draw it now. Remember Courser reveals the top — opponents see it.
- **Sterling Grove** ({1}, sac, top): free with a Sythis draw afterwards; also your answer to "Naturalize my Sanctum Weaver" — sac in response for value and it drops shroud at the same time. **Moon-Blessed Cleric** (ETB top): a 3-mana "draw the combo piece" with an enchantment cast afterwards; re-buyable with Excavation. **Hall of Heliod's Generosity:** dead Weaver/Gauntlets/Presence back on top. **Urza's Saga III:** Sol Ring only (Thought Vessel is MV 2; if Sol Ring is already out, chapter III finds nothing).
- **Nature's Rhythm** (creature MV ≤ X onto the battlefield): X=2 Sanctum Weaver / Argothian / Destiny Spinner / Weaver of Harmony / Herald / Jukai / Springheart / Composer / Shigeki / Starfield / Daxos; X=3 Courser / Katilda / Dryad / Cleric / Enduring Vitality; X=4 Eidolon / Arasta; X=5 Ondu / Elesh Norn; X=7 Nyxbloom. Harmonize from the graveyard later — tap a pumped Sythis to pay the generic part.
- **Bala Ged Recovery** (any card: Replenish, Gauntlets, Sol Ring), **Shigeki channel** ({X}{X}{G}{G}: X nonlegendary cards), **Replenish / Redress Fate** (everything). Redress Fate cannot be tutored.
- Tutor priority: (Weaver out) Gauntlets → (Gauntlets out) Weaver → kill outlet you lack (Excavation/Blind Obedience, Spinner) → Earthcraft when Luminarch is at 3–4 counters → Secret Arcade when Ondu is in hand → Sphere/Confinement when you are the target → Test of Endurance at 45+ life.

## 6. Mulligan
- **Keep:** 2–4 lands with a green source on turn 1, at least one ramp piece (Utopia Sprawl / Wild Growth / Fertile Ground / Sol Ring / Sanctum Weaver / Wolfwillow), and either an enchantress/reducer or 2+ cheap enchantments. Sythis in the command zone is your turn-2 engine, so a hand of *lands + ramp + cheap enchantments* is a keep even with no creature. Ideal: land, land, Sprawl, Argothian or Herald, two 1–2-mana enchantments, one protection spell.
- **Mulligan:** 0–1 lands; 5+ lands with nothing castable below 4; hands whose spells are Sphere / Nyxbloom / Elesh Norn / Ondu / Replenish and nothing cheap; no green source with only green 1-drops.
- **Gemstone Caverns** if you are not the starting player: start with it on the battlefield (exile the worst card in hand) → turn-1 two-drop (Weaver, Argothian, Herald, Jukai) and Sythis + a 1-drop on turn 2.
- Six-card hands with land + ramp + Sythis plan are fine; go to five before keeping a no-land or no-action seven.

## 7. Turn sequencing
- **T1:** untapped green land + Sprawl/Wild Growth/Fertile Ground (on a basic, name White with Sprawl) or Sol Ring; Alseid if nothing else. Don't lead Concordant Crossroads into an aggro table (it gives *everyone* haste) — it is a combo enabler, cast it the turn you need it, and Blind Obedience neutralizes it for them.
- **T2:** Sythis (with Sol Ring: Sythis + a 1-drop). Cast Sythis before the rest of the turn's enchantments.
- **T3:** second draw piece (Argothian / Presence / Eidolon) or reducer (Herald / Jukai / Starfield), then Sanctum Weaver, then every 1–2-mana enchantment you can afford. From here each enchantment should draw 2+.
- **Every turn, in this order:** (1) lands off the top (Courser) and land drop(s); (2) draw engines and reducers; (3) mana auras and Weaver; (4) fort/removal as needed; (5) tutor-to-top, then one more enchantment to draw it; (6) Tempest Technique / Spinner activations last. Keep a land in hand for Composer; keep a card for Confinement's upkeep.
- **Hold-up policy:** once the board is worth three or more cards, keep {1}{G} (Heroic Intervention) or {2}{W} (Teferi's Protection) open when a wrath deck is untapping — or keep Clever Concealment castable by convoke. Don't tap out into an obvious Farewell turn; if you must, bait with the cheapest enchantments (each still draws if countered).
- **Combat:** attack with Katilda / Shielded Sythis / Angels only into open boards or when a lifelink hit moves you toward 50; the deck wins off the stack and the upkeep, not by racing.
- **Fort timing:** Sphere of Safety once the table swings at you or you pass 8 enchantments; Solitary Confinement when you face burn, commander damage, or combat you can't block — only when Sythis is drawing (you skip your draw step). Curse of Exhaustion goes on the storm/counterspell seat early. Blind Obedience turn 2–3 against Treasure/haste decks.
- **Emergence Zone:** spend it for the end-step Test of Endurance, the end-step Luminarch Angel loop, an end-step Quarantine Field, or to deploy Gauntlets + win in response to a sorcery-speed threat.

## 8. Threat assessment across the pod
- **Who hurts you:** (1) mass artifact/enchantment removal and wrath decks (Farewell, Austere Command, Bane of Progress, Merciless Eviction) — don't overextend, hold Heroic/Teferi's/Concealment, keep Replenish; (2) counterspell decks — Destiny Spinner first (your creatures and enchantments become uncounterable), Curse of Exhaustion on them, bait with 1-drops; (3) fast combo — Grasp of Fate / Banishing Light / Quarantine Field on their engine, Blind Obedience (Treasures and hasty creatures enter tapped), Elesh Norn (their ETB combos — Thassa's Oracle, Dockside, Gray Merchant, Kiki copies — simply don't trigger), Curse of Exhaustion; (4) go-wide / aggro — Sphere, Confinement, Arasta, Blind Obedience, Eiganjo; (5) Voltron / commander damage — Solitary Confinement, Song of the Dryads or Kenrith's Transformation on the commander (it stays a 3/3 vanilla commander), Trickster's Elk bestowed on it; (6) targeted-removal-heavy — Privileged Position, Asceticism, Sterling Grove (mind the trap).
- **What to exile first with Grasp/Banishing/Quarantine:** Cursed Totem / Collector Ouphe / Linvala effects (stop Sanctum Weaver and Gauntlets), Rest in Peace (kills Replenish/Redress), Aven Mindcensor / Opposition Agent effects (your tutors), Drannith Magistrate, opposing stax that taxes activations, the engine permanent of the fastest deck, and the card that beats your fort (e.g., a fliers/evasion lord, a drain engine).
- **Aura Shards** is your repeatable answer: every creature you deploy (including tokens) may destroy an artifact or enchantment — sequence creatures when a target exists.
- **Politics:** you are the slow value deck; be boring until you are one turn from winning. Never be the first to expose Weaver + Gauntlets into open blue mana; assemble at an end step or behind Destiny Spinner + protection.

## 9. Win lines ranked, and what to protect
1. **Sanctum Weaver + Gauntlets of Light → extort kill (4.5)** or **Destiny Spinner alpha / Luminarch Angels / Hydra / Quarantine Field (4.1)**. Pieces: Weaver + Gauntlets (+ Blind Obedience + Excavation + Sol Ring, or Spinner). Protect Weaver above everything once Gauntlets is in hand; a bounced/dead Weaver is rebuyable (Excavation, Bala Ged, Hall of Heliod's, Shifting Woodland copy, Nature's Rhythm X=2).
2. **Earthcraft + Luminarch (4 counters) + aura'd basic (4.2)** at the last opponent's end step → N Angels; Daxos/Aura Shards/Cradle outlets; swing next turn.
3. **Secret Arcade + Ondu (4.3)** → N enchantment 3/3s; Crossroads or Cradle/Spinner to convert.
4. **Excavation + Weaver + Crossroads (4.4)** → draw into line 1, wipe their artifacts/enchantments, Parlor-counter Sythis, reach 50.
5. **Test of Endurance** at 50+ behind Teferi's Protection / Clever Concealment / Emergence Zone end-step cast.
6. **Voltron:** Sythis + Shield of the Oversoul (+ Rising Dawn / Strength / Tempest Technique / Parlor counters / Hydra) for 21; Katilda as the lifelink beater.
- **Protect, in order:** Sanctum Weaver (Gift of Immortality, Shield — she is green so it's indestructible, Asceticism, Privileged Position, Heroic, Excavation-bounce on your turn); Sythis (cheap to recast, but tax climbs — Timely Ward at flash, Shield, Gift, Alseid, Asceticism); the draw pieces (Argothian already has shroud); Luminarch once it has 2+ counters; Gauntlets/Earthcraft only while the loop is live.
- **Never:** loop past your library (count before you start); cast Sterling Grove before the Auras you still want on your creatures; put Utopia Sprawl on a land that is a Forest only because of Dryad/Yavimaya when a real Forest is available; crack Windswept Heath or shock Temple Garden on an opponent's turn while Luminarch is charging; give Alseid protection from a color that knocks off your own Aura of that color; decline Solitary Confinement's discard while it is still your only defense; tap the lands you mean to animate with Destiny Spinner.

## 10. Quick reference
- Reducer math (3 reducers = Herald + Jukai + Starfield): Sphere {1}{W}; Secret Arcade cast {1}{W} (unlock still {4}{W}); Gauntlets {W}; Quarantine Field X=2 {1}{W}{W}, X=3 {3}{W}{W}; Katilda disturb {W}{W}; Hydra X=5 {2}{G}; Springheart bestow {G}; Ondu {1}{W} only with Arcade out.
- Gauntlets loop net = X − 3 per cycle (3X − 3 with Nyxbloom). Excavation–Weaver loop net = X − 5 (X − 4 with a reducer). Luminarch–Earthcraft loop: +0 with 2-mana basics, +1 with Overgrowth/Nyxbloom.
- Loop caps: 4.1 and 4.5 unbounded (no draws, unless Arcade makes the rebuy an enchantment spell); 4.2 unbounded unless Arcade + Eidolon; 4.3 capped by Eidolon draws if Eidolon is out; 4.4 always capped by library.
- Earthcraft: basics only (4 Forest, 4 Plains, Heath fetches one); summoning-sick creatures can pay. Weaver of Harmony: no mana abilities. Ondu: once per Ondu per turn; copied Rooms locked, copied Quarantine Field X=0, copied legends die. Destiny Spinner: X locked on resolution. Luminarch: life lost on *your* turn doesn't matter. Sythis bounced to hand recasts tax-free. Test of Endurance trigger on the stack is only stopped by life loss below 50.
