## Overview

Mono-white Angel tribal led by Giada, Font of Hope: overwhelming board of
large synergistic Angels via Giada's cost reduction and +1/+1 counters,
winning through combat — plus a potent infinite combo (Heliod, Sun-Crowned +
Walking Ballista), stax pieces, and land destruction (Armageddon / Ravages
of War) to protect the developing board.

## Core strategy

- T1-3: fast mana (Sol Ring, Mana Vault, Mox Diamond, Ancient Tomb); Giada
  ASAP; value engines (Esper Sentinel, Land Tax, Smothering Tithe).
- T3-5: Angels compound (Resplendent Angel, Righteous Valkyrie, Lyra,
  Archangel of Thune); tutors (Enlightened/Idyllic Tutor, Urza's Saga) find
  Smothering Tithe, Luminarch Ascension, or combo pieces; stax (Drannith
  Magistrate, Grand Abolisher, Linvala, Angel of Jubilation).
- T4-8 wins:
  - COMBAT: Avacyn indestructibility + alpha strike amplified by Akroma's
    Will.
  - COMBO: Heliod + Walking Ballista — Ballista pings, Heliod grants it
    lifelink for {1}{W}, damage → lifegain → Heliod counter back on
    Ballista → repeat for infinite damage.
  - LOCK: Armageddon / Ravages of War from a winning board.

## Mulligan priorities (human rubric)

- 2-3 lands (white source), at least one fast-mana source, early value
  engine or tutor; early protection (Mother/Giver of Runes) in interactive
  pods.

## Key tips

- Protect Giada (Greaves, Runes mothers, Flawless Maneuver class).
- Stax deployed proactively but mind self-hits (Angel of Jubilation vs Mox
  Diamond/Ancient Tomb).
- Lifegain synergy grows the board (Thune, Resplendent, Valkyrie).
- Land destruction only from a clear advantage.
- The One Ring early; Urza's Saga fetches Sol Ring/Ballista/Greaves.

## Weaknesses

- CRITICAL: mass exile/bounce (Farewell, Cyclonic Rift) bypasses
  indestructibility; activated-ability stax (Cursed Totem, Null Rod) kills
  rocks + Heliod/Ballista + Nykthos.
- MODERATE: targeted removal on Giada/Avacyn/Thune/combo; counterspell
  metas; graveyard hate vs minor recursion.
- MINOR: faster go-wide decks; rule-of-law effects.

## Key cards

Giada (commander/ramp/anthem); Heliod, Sun-Crowned + Walking Ballista
(combo); Smothering Tithe; The One Ring; Avacyn; Archangel of Thune;
Enlightened Tutor; Sol Ring; Land Tax.

## Attribute ratings (DeckCheck)

- Speed 7/10 — T4-5 combo possible, combat typically T6-8.
- Resilience 7/10 — protection suite; vulnerable to mass exile/bounce.
- Consistency 8/10 — fast mana density, tutors, draw engines.
- Interaction 6/10 — strong permanent removal + stax; thin stack presence.
- Power level 7.0-7.5.
