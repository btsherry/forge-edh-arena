# Purphoros, God of the Forge — DeckCheck primer (external reference)

> Source: deckcheck.co analysis of this list, pasted by Ben 2026-07-17.
> Reference material for pilot-decision grading (plan §7 decision audits) and
> Gate 1.5 calibration. NOT a prep artifact — the dossier never reads this.

## Overview

This mono-red deck is a classic Purphoros, God of the Forge ETB-damage engine
that floods the board with creatures and tokens to ping the table for lethal.
The core plan deploys Purphoros (or parallel damage engines like Terror of the
Peaks and Agate Instigator), then generates repeated enters-the-battlefield
triggers via myriad attackers (Warchief Giant, Elturel Survivors, Genasi
Enforcers, Goldlust Triad), Song of Totentanz, Kher Keep, and copy effects.
Damage amplifiers (Torbran, Thane of Red Fell, Solphim, Mayhem Dominus,
Twinflame Tyrant, Ojer Axonil, Deepest Might) turn modest token waves into
table-wide kills. Secondary paths include Dualcaster Mage copy loops for
infinite hasty bodies and ritual-fueled storm turns with wheels and Jeska's
Will. The commander is a powerful but non-essential damage outlet; the 99
contains fully independent combo and combat lines.

## Core strategy

- Accelerate with cheap rocks (Sol Ring, Mana Vault, Chrome Mox, Ruby
  Medallion) and rituals (Jeska's Will, Mana Geyser, Treasonous Ogre) while
  deploying early value creatures (Ragavan, Norin the Wary, Runaway Steam-Kin).
- Land a damage engine — ideally Purphoros, otherwise Terror of the Peaks,
  Agate Instigator, or Solphim, Mayhem Dominus — then start producing bodies.
- Generate mass ETBs: Song of Totentanz for large X, myriad attacks, or loop
  Dualcaster Mage with Twinflame / Reiterate / Flare of Duplication / Return
  the Favor for infinite hasty Dualcasters.
- Amplify and close: Torbran / Twinflame Tyrant / Ojer Axonil stack so each
  ETB deals lethal table damage; or swing with infinite Dualcasters; Neheb,
  the Eternal funds a second storm turn.
- Protect the turn with Deflecting Swat or Spider-Punk; refill via wheels
  (Wheel of Fortune, Reforge the Soul, Wheel of Misfortune) or The One Ring.

## Mulligan priorities (human rubric)

- KEEP: 2-3 lands + fast mana (Sol Ring, Mana Vault, Chrome Mox, Ruby
  Medallion) or a 1-drop engine (Ragavan, Norin the Wary, Goldhound); hands
  with Jeska's Will or a wheel plus ramp are premium.
- AVOID: high-curve hands without acceleration, pure land-heavy draws, or
  multiple expensive myriad creatures with no early action.

## Key tips

- Sequence Norin the Wary / Grinning Ignus after Purphoros is online for free
  repeated pings.
- Imperial Recruiter fetches Dualcaster Mage, Agate Instigator, or Norin.
- Cursed Mirror / Devastating Onslaught copy the best damage engine or create
  token armies that still trigger Purphoros/Terror.
- Hold Deflecting Swat / Chaos Warp for wipes or hate; Vandalblast overloaded
  clears artifact stax.
- After a wheel, chain free/discounted red spells with Birgi or Runaway
  Steam-Kin out.
- Ojer Axonil floors all noncombat damage at 4, turning small pings lethal
  once amplifiers are present.

## Weaknesses

- CRITICAL: mass creature exile / one-sided wipes that also hit tokens
  (Farewell, Merciless Eviction) — few recursion paths beyond Pinnacle Monk
  and flashback.
- MODERATE: graveyard hate slows the flashback/harmonize package but not the
  primary ETB plan; rule-of-law / hard stax taxing creature casts or activated
  abilities hinders token generation and Dualcaster loops (limited enchantment
  removal).
- MINOR: self-damage (Ancient Tomb, Treasonous Ogre, The One Ring) under
  concurrent pressure; single-target removal on amplifiers annoying but
  rarely game-ending (redundant engines).

## Key cards

Dualcaster Mage (copies Twinflame/Reiterate/Flare of Duplication for infinite
hasty creatures); Twinflame; Song of Totentanz (X tokens, all trigger the
engines); Torbran, Thane of Red Fell (+2 amplifier); Solphim, Mayhem Dominus
(doubles noncombat damage); Terror of the Peaks; Agate Instigator; Jeska's
Will; Norin the Wary; Birgi, God of Storytelling; Deflecting Swat; Wheel of
Fortune; Ojer Axonil, Deepest Might; Imperial Recruiter.

## Attribute ratings (DeckCheck)

- Consistency 6.75/10 — modest tutor density (Gamble, Imperial Recruiter,
  Reckless Handling, Urza's Saga); strong wheels/impulse draw can't fully
  compensate.
- Resilience 10/10 — nine distinct combo lines entirely in the 99, commander
  non-essential; flashback/harmonize/Pinnacle Monk recursion; interlocking
  inevitability.
- Interaction 5.25/10 — functional but telegraphed; stack presence is
  basically Deflecting Swat.
- Speed 6.5/10 — median goldfish kill turns 5-6: engine by turn 3-4, then
  Jeska's Will/wheels + Song of Totentanz or a Dualcaster line closes.
