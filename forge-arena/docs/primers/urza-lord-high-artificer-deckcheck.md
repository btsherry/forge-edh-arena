# Urza, Lord High Artificer — DeckCheck primer (external reference)

> Source: deckcheck.co analysis of this list, pasted by Ben 2026-07-17.
> Reference material for pilot-decision grading (plan §7 decision audits) and
> Gate 1.5 calibration. NOT a prep artifact — the dossier never reads this.

## Overview

Mono-blue artifact storm around Urza, Lord High Artificer as engine, win
condition, and primary mana source. Assemble infinite mana (Isochron Scepter
+ Dramatic Reversal; Power Artifact on Grim/Basalt Monolith), then win via
Urza's {5} ability exiling the library and casting the win spells, Nexus of
Fate loops, or Hullbreaker Horror / Tidespout Tyrant bounce locks. Urza is
indispensable; the deck is vulnerable to commander removal but layers
protection and redundancy.

## Core strategy

- T0-2: fast mana (Mox Diamond, Chrome Mox, Mana Vault, Sol Ring) + cost
  reducers (Etherium Sculptor, Foundry Inspector); land Urza early via Cavern
  of Souls or protection.
- T3-4: cast Urza, make Constructs, tap artifacts for blue; tutor combo
  pieces (Transmute Artifact, Fabricate, Solve the Equation) or engines
  (Rhystic Study, Consecrated Sphinx); Unwinding Clock untaps on opponents'
  turns.
- T4-5 combos: Scepter+Reversal infinite mana (3+ nonland sources); Power
  Artifact + Monolith infinite colorless; Hullbreaker/Tidespout bounce
  domination; Nexus of Fate + Academy Ruins infinite turns.
- Win/lock: Urza's {5} repeatedly with infinite mana exiles the library →
  Thassa's Oracle equivalents, or Static/Winter Orb + Urza lock.

## Mulligan priorities (human rubric)

- KEEP: 2+ lands (blue sources), 1-2 fast-mana artifacts, and Urza or a
  tutor; prioritize protection (Force of Will, Fierce Guardianship) in
  interactive metas.
- AVOID: hands lacking early acceleration or blue mana.

## Key tips

- Protect Urza: Greaves immediately, hold Swan Song/free counters.
- Mishra's Workshop for early artifact casts; Nykthos for devotion spikes.
- Academy Ruins / Buried Ruin recur pieces; Emry casts from graveyard.
- Minamo untaps Urza; Unwinding Clock bypasses stax.

## Weaknesses

- CRITICAL: artifact hate (Collector Ouphe, Stony Silence, Null Rod);
  repeated Urza removal (3+) stalls irrecoverably; graveyard hate (RIP) cuts
  Academy Ruins/Emry/Nexus recursion.
- MODERATE: Opposition Agent / Aven Mindcensor vs tutors; heavy counterspell
  decks; rule-of-law effects.
- MINOR: nonbasic land hate; hand disruption.

## Key cards

Urza (commander/engine/win); Isochron Scepter + Dramatic Reversal (primary
infinite mana); Power Artifact (Monolith line); Transmute Artifact; Mishra's
Workshop; Hullbreaker Horror; Force of Will; Academy Ruins; Nexus of Fate.

## Attribute ratings (DeckCheck)

- Speed 9/10 — T3 wins possible (T1 Workshop → T2 Urza → T3 Scepter/
  Reversal); consistently threatens T4-5.
- Resilience 8/10 — free countermagic, recursion, redundant lines; heavy
  Urza reliance.
- Consistency 9/10 — 10+ tutors, strong draw engines, interchangeable wins.
- Interaction 8/10 — free counters, Mana Drain, targeted removal, stax.
- Power level 8.5-9.0 — cEDH-viable, T2-3 benchmarks; artifact-hate and
  Urza-reliance keep it below Tier 0/1.

## Arena notes (2026-07-17)

The strongest future generality test in the pod: the Scepter+Reversal shape
is an imprint-copy untap loop (a cousin of TapForManaUntapLoop with a
spell-copy untapper — likely a new archetype or a TapForMana generalization),
Urza's library-exile win is an ORACLE_WIN-class route, and the deck's cEDH
speed would pressure-test the pilot's protection/patience layers. Candidate
fourth archetype family after SpellCopyLoop.

## Mana discipline (2026-08-17, from live-game evidence)

- This deck's mana is COLOURLESS-HEAVY: most rocks and several lands cannot
  pay {U}. Count your actual blue sources before committing to a {U}{U}+
  spell (Counterspell, Mana Drain, Hullbreaker Horror's protection window):
  the two recorded misses were both "{U}{U} with one blue source".
- Urza's tap-an-artifact ability is a blue source ONLY while Urza is on the
  battlefield, and each activation costs you an untapped artifact — count it
  as blue-per-untapped-artifact, not as a standing blue land.
- Mishra's Workshop pays for ARTIFACT spells only — never count it toward
  instants, sorceries, or Urza himself.
