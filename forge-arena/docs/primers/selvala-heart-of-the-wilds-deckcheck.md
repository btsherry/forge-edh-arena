## Overview

This mono-green Selvala deck is a high-power stompy-combo hybrid that ramps
aggressively into oversized creatures, then weaponizes Selvala, Heart of the
Wilds as both a mana battery and card-advantage engine. The plan floods the
board with dorks, land auras, and powerhouses so Selvala's tap ability produces
enormous colored mana while her ETB trigger refills the hand. Primary wins come
from infinite-mana loops (Selvala plus Umbral Mantle or Staff of Domination)
that dump the library via Greater Good, Genesis Wave, or Finale of Devastation,
or from a classic Craterhoof Behemoth / Finale of Devastation overrun.
Secondary lines include Phyrexian Dreadnought cheating, Temur Sabertooth value
loops, and pure combat with trample lords. The commander is central for
acceleration and draw but the 99 still contains independent big-mana and combat
payoffs.

## Core strategy

- Early ramp: 1-drop dorks (Arbor Elf, Delighted Halfling, Allosaurus Shepherd,
  Magus of the Candelabra) and cheap auras (Wild Growth, Utopia Sprawl, Fertile
  Ground, Overgrowth) while hitting land drops that include Gaea's Cradle,
  Nykthos, Shrine to Nyx, or Ancient Tomb.
- Cast Selvala on curve (ideally turn 3); tap for mana equal to highest power
  on board, draw off every larger creature that enters.
- Assemble: tutor or draw into Umbral Mantle / Staff of Domination plus any
  4+ power body for infinite mana, or into Craterhoof / Finale X≥10. Temur
  Sabertooth or Seedborn Muse protect and re-use ETBs.
- Close: infinite mana fuels Genesis Wave, Greater Good + outlet, or a one-shot
  Craterhoof / Finale that ends the table. Concordant Crossroads or haste
  enablers ensure the swing connects the same turn.

## Mulligan priorities (human rubric)

- KEEP: 2-3 lands (ideally one special), a 1-drop dork or aura, and either
  Selvala protection (Lightning Greaves, Swiftfoot Boots) or a tutor (Green
  Sun's Zenith, Archdruid's Charm).
- AVOID: hands with only high-CMC bombs, no ramp, or pure interaction with no
  acceleration.

## Key tips

- Sequence auras onto Gaea's Cradle or basics that Arbor Elf / Voyaging Satyr
  can untap for explosive turns.
- Hold Heroic Intervention / Collective Resistance for the combo or Craterhoof
  turn; Dosan the Falling Leaf locks the stack on the combo turn.
- Phyrexian Dreadnought plus any 12-power total (or Sheltering Ancient) is a
  free 12/12 that immediately enables Selvala mana and draw.
- Earthcraft plus any token or dork engine turns basic lands into infinite mana
  once you have a large creature.
- Saryth, the Viper's Fang or Asceticism keep Selvala and key pieces safe.
- If Selvala is removed, pivot to Finale of Devastation or Nature's Rhythm for
  a big creature and win through combat.

## Weaknesses

- CRITICAL: heavy battlefield reliance — a well-timed wipe after Selvala and
  the big creatures are out forces a slow rebuild with limited mass recursion.
- MODERATE: graveyard hate slows Eternal Witness / Bala Ged Recovery /
  Shifting Woodland lines; artifact/enchantment hate (Collector Ouphe,
  Vandalblast) blanks Mantle/Staff and ramp auras (answers: Reclamation Sage,
  Beast Within, Song of the Dryads, Boseiju); commander tax escalates if
  Selvala is repeatedly removed before protection is online.
- MINOR: colorless sinks/X-spells awkward under stax; no counterspells — race
  or rely on Destiny Spinner / Allosaurus Shepherd uncounterability.

## Key cards

Umbral Mantle (core infinite-mana outlet, power 4+); Staff of Domination
(alternate infinite mana + draw); Craterhoof Behemoth (premier finisher);
Finale of Devastation (tutor + overrun, X≥10 lethal); Green Sun's Zenith
(premier tutor); Greater Good (oversized creatures → card advantage / combo
fuel); Seedborn Muse (untap everything each turn); Temur Sabertooth (bounce
protection + ETB re-use); Gaea's Cradle; Phyrexian Dreadnought (near-free
12/12); Earthcraft (creatures → mana dorks, infinite land untaps); Heroic
Intervention (combo-turn protection); Archdruid's Charm (instant tutor /
removal); The Great Henge (draw engine + rock); Concordant Crossroads (board
haste for same-turn kills).

## Attribute ratings (DeckCheck)

- Consistency 7/10 — tutor suite (GSZ, Finale, Nature's Rhythm, Archdruid's
  Charm, Invasion of Ikoria) + dense interchangeable ramp/big-creature
  packages; capped by real search density.
- Resilience 7/10 — eleven distinct combo lines (Selvala-Mantle/Staff,
  Earthcraft engines, Dreadnought lines, Craterhoof/Finale overruns, Sabertooth
  value) + recursion + protection; the 99 functions at roughly half-to-three-
  quarters capacity without the commander.
- Interaction 5.25/10 — ~a dozen interactive pieces, no counterspells or free
  interaction; telegraphed and reactive.
- Speed 7/10 — median goldfish win on turn 5: T1 land+dork, T2 land+aura/ramp,
  T3 Selvala, T4 high-power creature or tutor into Mantle/Staff/Craterhoof,
  T5 infinite mana or lethal overrun.
