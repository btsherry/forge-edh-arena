# Syr Gwyn, Voltron Hero — DeckCheck analysis

- **Commander:** Syr Gwyn, Hero of Ashvale
- **Bracket:** 3
- **CRISPI:** consistency 6.75 · interaction 5.75 · resilience 5.0 · speed 6.5 · performance 6.0

## Overview:

This Mardu Equipment deck focuses on aggressive Voltron combat, converting high-impact weapons into rapid lethality while using Syr Gwyn, Hero of Ashvale to cheat high equip costs onto Knights and draw cards on attack. The primary win condition is eliminating opponents with massive commander damage or heavily equipped double strike threats like Balan, Wandering Knight and Kellan, the Fae-Blooded. Equipping massive stat boosters such as Colossus Hammer, Kaldra Compleat, or Embercleave allows the deck to knock out players in single swings. Secondary win lines include taking consecutive combat steps with Genji Glove or amassing an alternate artifact win via Knuckles the Echidna.

## Primer:

**Core Strategy:**

-

Deploy early equipment support and cost reducers like Puresteel Paladin, Sigarda's Aid, or Ardenn, Intrepid Archaeologist alongside cheap ramp.

-

Tutor for key high-impact equipment such as Colossus Hammer, Kaldra Compleat, or Hammer of Nazahn using Stoneforge Mystic, Fighter Class, or Cloud, Midgar Mercenary.

-

Cast Syr Gwyn, Hero of Ashvale or an alternate knight threat, attach multiple heavy weapons for free, and swing to draw cards and eliminate opponents sequentially through evasive commander damage.

**Mulligan Priorities:**

-

**Keep:** Hands with 3 lands, at least one early engine or tutor piece such as Puresteel Paladin, Sigarda's Aid, or Stoneforge Mystic, and cheap equipment to curve out naturally.

-

**Avoid:** Hands without early white or red mana sources, hands loaded with expensive equipment above 4 mana without equip-cost cheat engines, or hands lacking creature threats.

**Key Tips:**

-

Use Sigarda's Aid or Forge Anew to attach equipment like Colossus Hammer or Embercleave at instant speed right after blockers are declared.

-

Hold phasing and protection spells like Galadriel's Dismissal, Flare of Fortitude, or Teferi's Protection specifically to protect a fully equipped threat from mass exile or sacrifice.

-

Even if Syr Gwyn is not in play, cheat heavy equip costs using Ardenn, Intrepid Archaeologist, Codsworth, Handy Helper, or Puresteel Paladin to pressure life totals.

## Weaknesses:

**Critical**

- Mass artifact removal spells such as Farewell or Vandalblast wipe out the vast majority of the deck's board presence, equipment investment, and primary win conditions.

**Moderate**

-

Heavily targeted creature removal can stall momentum when the deck lacks instant-speed attach effects or equip cost reducers to re-equip newly cast creatures.

-

Null Rod and Collector Ouphe effects disable activated abilities of equipment, shutting down manual equip costs and utility artifacts like The One Ring.

**Minor**

- Chump blockers can temporarily delay single-target combat kills if trample or unblockable equipment like Shadowspear or Brotherhood Regalia is not found.

## Key Cards:

-

**Sigarda's Aid:** Provides instant-speed equipment deployment and bypasses equip costs entirely on entry.

-

**Colossus Hammer:** Supplies +10/+10 of raw power to enable one-shot commander kills when equip costs are cheated.

-

**Puresteel Paladin:** Acts as both a potent card draw engine and a source of zero-cost equipping via Metalcraft.

-

**Stoneforge Mystic:** Tutors any piece of equipment directly to hand and cheats high-cost equipment onto the battlefield.

-

**Ardenn, Intrepid Archaeologist:** Consolidates all equipment onto a single attacker at the start of combat for zero mana.

-

**Hammer of Nazahn:** Automatically attaches newly entering equipment and gives the equipped creature indestructible.

-

**Balan, Wandering Knight:** Serves as an explosive secondary win condition that gains double strike and equips everything instantly.

-

**Kaldra Compleat:** Provides a resilient, hasty, and indestructible threat that grants overwhelming offensive keywords.

-

**Forge Anew:** Recurs destroyed equipment from the graveyard and allows free equip activations at instant speed.
