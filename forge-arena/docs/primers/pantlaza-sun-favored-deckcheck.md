# Pantlaza, Sun-Favored — DeckCheck analysis

- **Commander:** Pantlaza, Sun-Favored
- **Bracket:** 3
- **CRISPI:** consistency 7.0 · interaction 7.0 · resilience 5.0 · speed 6.0 · performance 6.25

## Overview:

This deck is a high-impact Naya stompy list commanded by Pantlaza, Sun-Favored. The game plan focuses on rapid ramp, deploying massive Dinosaurs, and cascading deep into the library via the discover mechanic to quickly overwhelm the table. Early turns develop heavy mana using creatures and spells before casting Pantlaza to trigger discover. Pantlaza continuously replaces herself and chains massive threats like Gishath, Sun's Avatar, Etali, Primal Conqueror, and Ghalta, Stampede Tyrant onto the board. You win primarily through devastating, wide combat steps granted by haste and evasion enablers like Rhythm of the Wild, Akroma's Memorial, and Finale of Devastation.

## Primer:

**Core Strategy:**

-

Accelerate early with cheap ramp like Birds of Paradise, Nature's Lore, Three Visits, and Bloom Tender to deploy Pantlaza, Sun-Favored on turn three or four.

-

Trigger Pantlaza's discover ability by landing tough Dinosaurs or flickering them with Ephemerate and Emiel the Blessed to chain free spells and build an overwhelming board.

-

Drop game-ending threats such as Gishath, Sun's Avatar, Ghalta, Stampede Tyrant, or cast a massive Finale of Devastation to wipe out all opponents in a single combat step.

**Mulligan Priorities:**

-

**Keep:** Hands with two to three lands, all three colors represented, and at least one to two cheap ramp spells like Birds of Paradise, Three Visits, or Farseek-style effects to ensure an early Pantlaza.

-

**Avoid:** Hands full of high-mana Dinosaurs without early green ramp, or hands missing green mana entirely.

**Key Tips:**

-

Remember that Pantlaza triggers only once each turn; use instant flicker spells like Ephemerate on an opponent's turn to get additional discover triggers.

-

Savage Order sacrificing a power 4 or greater creature like Pantlaza or Topiary Stomper can tutor an indestructible Apex Altisaur or Gishath, Sun's Avatar directly into play.

-

Hold up free or low-cost protection spells such as Deflecting Swat, Flare of Fortitude, or Heroic Intervention before committing game-ending board states into open mana.

## Weaknesses:

**Critical**

- No critical weakness identified.

**Moderate**

-

Heavy board wipes and sacrifice effects set the deck back significantly if protection spells like Heroic Intervention or Teferi's Protection are not held up.

-

Rule of Law and strict spell-count limiters disrupt the deck's ability to chain free spells off discover triggers.

**Minor**

-

Targeted graveyard hate mildly diminishes value from Bala Ged Recovery, Eternal Witness, and Sevinne's Reclamation.

-

A high density of high-mana cards can cause occasional clunky starting hands without early ramp.

## Key Cards:

-

**Gishath, Sun's Avatar:** Provides an immediate, high-impact combat threat that cheats multiple Dinosaurs onto the battlefield on hit.

-

**Etali, Primal Conqueror // Etali, Primal Sickness:** Exiles and casts free spells from every player's deck and serves as an alternate poison win condition.

-

**Ghalta, Stampede Tyrant:** Dumps your entire hand of massive creature threats onto the battlefield simultaneously.

-

**Savage Order:** Tutors any Dinosaur directly onto the battlefield with temporary indestructible at sorcery speed.

-

**Emiel the Blessed:** Repeatedly flickers Dinosaurs to reuse enter-the-battlefield abilities and trigger Pantlaza on each player's turn.

-

**Finale of Devastation:** Serves as a flexible creature tutor early or an overrun finisher giving all creatures +10/+10 and haste.

-

**The Great Henge:** Provides huge mana acceleration, +1/+1 counters, life gain, and repeatable card draw on creature ETBs.

-

**Akroma's Memorial:** Grants haste, flying, trample, and key protections across the entire board to swing for lethal immediately.

## Attribute Ratings:

**Consistency: 7/10**

- The deck runs 35 tutor points including Green Sun's Zenith, Eladamri's Call, and Savage Order alongside 53 draw points to reliably access ramp and top-end Dinosaurs. The land count of 34 is backed by abundant two-mana ramp spells, keeping the plan on curve.

**Resilience: 5/10**

- Defensive spells like Heroic Intervention, Flare of Fortitude, and Teferi's Protection pair with targeted recursion like Eternal Witness and Bala Ged Recovery to protect and rebuild board states. Creature-based card advantage engines such as Vaultborn Tyrant ensure you do not run out of gas.

**Interaction: 7/10**

- The deck features 36 interactive pieces with broad coverage across creatures, artifacts, and enchantments like Kogla and Yidaro, Bronzebeak Foragers, and Beast Within. Instant-speed options and flexible answers like Archdruid's Charm allow active board control.

**Speed: 6/10**

- A typical game curves turn 1 mana dork into turn 2 ramp, turn 3 Pantlaza discovering a mid-cost threat, and turn 4 dropping a heavy engine like The Great Henge or Savage Order into Gishath, Sun's Avatar. By turn 6, combat damage with trample and haste via Akroma's Memorial or Finale of Devastation eliminates the table. The high concentration of four-plus mana value Dinosaurs creates a natural turn-6 median goldfish window.
