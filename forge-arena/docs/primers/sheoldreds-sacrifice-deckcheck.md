# Sheoldred's Sacrifice — DeckCheck analysis

- **Commander:** Sheoldred // The True Scriptures
- **Bracket:** 4
- **CRISPI:** consistency 6.75 · interaction 5.25 · resilience 9.0 · speed 5.0 · performance 6.5

## Overview:

Sheoldred commands a mono-black attrition and combo control deck that grinds down opposing boards through symmetric sacrifice effects, discard, and graveyard theft. The front face clears opposing threats on entry and transforms into The True Scriptures once opponents have full graveyards, culminating in a game-ending mass reanimation chapter. While Sheoldred provides immense value and board control, the primary win conditions stem from efficient two-card combinations and overwhelming mana advantage. The deck seals victories by assembling Bloodchief Ascension with Mindcrank, looping Mikaeus, the Unhallowed with Walking Ballista and a sacrifice outlet, or draining tables with Gray Merchant of Asphodel loops.

## Primer:

**Core Strategy:**

-

Develop early mana acceleration and card draw using Sol Ring, Jet Medallion, Black Market Connections, or Necropotence while establishing chip discard and attrition.

-

Control the board with repeated sacrifice triggers from Plaguecrafter, Fleshbag Marauder, Grave Pact, and Sheoldred to strip away opposing commanders and threats.

-

Assemble a game-ending combo such as Bloodchief Ascension paired with Mindcrank or Mikaeus, the Unhallowed with Walking Ballista, or cast Breach the Multiverse to overwhelm remaining life totals.

**Mulligan Priorities:**

-

**Keep:** Hands containing three lands, early mana ramp such as Sol Ring or Jet Medallion, and a reliable card draw engine like Black Market Connections or Skullclamp.

-

**Avoid:** Hands without early black mana, hands overloaded with expensive top-end spells like Archon of Cruelty and Breach the Multiverse, or hands lacking early interaction and card draw.

**Key Tips:**

-

Hold Bloodchief Ascension until you can reliably trigger its quest counters, as dropping it too early without pressure invites premature removal.

-

Pair Tergrid, God of Fright with forced sacrifice creatures like Demon's Disciple or Accursed Marauder to steal high-value opposing permanents immediately.

-

Use Phyrexian Tower and Ashnod's Altar to convert token fodder into burst mana for large plays like Yawgmoth's Will or Beseech the Mirror.

## Weaknesses:

**Critical**

- No critical weakness identified.

**Moderate**

-

Persistent graveyard hate like Rest in Peace or Dauthi Voidwalker disables your reanimation lines, Yawgmoth's Will, and threshold requirements while shutting down The True Scriptures.

-

Fast spell-based combo decks exploit your reliance on sorcery-speed creature edicts, as the deck runs almost no stack interaction or countermagic.

**Minor**

- Heavy life loss from cards like Necropotence, Toxic Deluge, and Shadowgrange Archfiend leaves you vulnerable to aggressive creature decks if lifegain engines are delayed.

## Key Cards:

-

**Bloodchief Ascension:** Provides massive life drain and forms an immediate infinite damage and mill combo when active alongside Mindcrank.

-

**Mindcrank:** Mills opposing libraries on life loss and combines with Bloodchief Ascension to end the game on any damage trigger.

-

**Mikaeus, the Unhallowed:** Grants undying to the board and acts as a combo piece alongside Walking Ballista and a sacrifice outlet.

-

**Walking Ballista:** Functions as targeted removal, an outlet for large mana pools, and a damage loop engine with Mikaeus.

-

**Necropotence:** Refills your hand with explosive card advantage to dig for combo pieces and answers.

-

**Tergrid, God of Fright // Tergrid's Lantern:** Steals every permanent opponents are forced to sacrifice or discard via your edict creatures.

-

**Cabal Coffers:** Generates enormous amounts of black mana when combined with Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth or basic Swamps.

-

**Breach the Multiverse:** Mills all players and steals the best four threats from graveyards for an overwhelming board state.

-

**Toxic Deluge:** Cheap, flexible mass creature removal that bypasses indestructible and wipes wide boards.

## Attribute Ratings:

**Consistency: 6.75/10**

- The deck leverages thirty tutor points including Demonic Tutor and Beseech the Mirror alongside fifty-one draw points from engines like Necropotence, The One Ring, and Skullclamp. This redundancy ensures smooth land drops and steady access to core combo pieces across games.

**Resilience: 9/10**

- With thirty-five weighted recursion points and sixteen rebuild engines like Yawgmoth's Will, Balthor the Defiled, and The Soul Stone, the deck effortlessly recovers from board wipes. Multiple independent win paths prevent single-point failure.

**Interaction: 5.25/10**

- The list features thirty-five interaction pieces focused predominantly on creature sacrifice, edicts, and board wipes. However, interaction is held back by containing only seven instant-speed answers and zero counterspells.

**Speed: 5/10**

- The deck establishes mana and card draw on turns one through three using Jet Medallion or Black Market Connections, drops a tutor or control piece like Grave Pact on turn four, casts Sheoldred or a large engine on turn five, and generates sufficient mana via Cabal Coffers or tutors on turn six. Turn seven consistently delivers the win via Bloodchief Ascension with Mindcrank activation or an undying Mikaeus loop. The turn-seven ceiling is governed by mana setup and multi-card assembly requirements without fast land ramp.
