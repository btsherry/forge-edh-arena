# Thalia and the Gitrog Monster - A Girl and Her Frog — DeckCheck analysis

- **Commander:** Thalia and The Gitrog Monster
- **Bracket:** 3
- **CRISPI:** consistency 7.0 · interaction 6.0 · resilience 8.0 · speed 5.5 · performance 6.63

## Overview:

Thalia and The Gitrog Monster commands an Abzan lands midrange deck that leverages aggressive land drops, graveyard recursion, and stax elements to overwhelm the table. By slowing opponent creature and nonbasic mana development, the commander secures breathing room to deploy ramp and self-mill engines like Life from the Loam and Aftermath Analyst. Massive land sacrifice and graveyard recovery effects trigger huge waves of tokens from Field of the Dead, Titania, Protector of Argoth, or Scute Swarm, while direct landfall damage from Retreat to Hagra or Ob Nixilis drains opponents. The deck closes games via overwhelming wide combat with Craterhoof Behemoth, mass drain, or explosive Scapeshift and Splendid Reclamation turns.

## Primer:

**Core Strategy:**

-

Develop your mana base early using cheap ramp like Nature's Lore, Three Visits, or Lotus Cobra to cast Thalia and The Gitrog Monster on turn three.

-

Fill your graveyard using cards like Life from the Loam, Satyr Wayfinder, and Grisly Salvage to stock lands and powerful reanimation targets.

-

Establish token and value engines like Field of the Dead, The Necrobloom, and Titania, Protector of Argoth while sacrificing and recurring lands.

-

Execute a game-ending turn by casting Scapeshift, Splendid Reclamation, or Lumra, Bellow of the Woods to trigger lethal landfall damage or an overwhelming Craterhoof Behemoth attack.

**Mulligan Priorities:**

-

**Keep:** Hands with three lands capable of producing all three Abzan colors, accompanied by a two-mana ramp spell or cheap self-mill enabler like Life from the Loam or Satyr Wayfinder.

-

**Avoid:** Hands missing green mana, containing only high-cost top-end payoffs like Craterhoof Behemoth or Eerie Ultimatum, or lacking early land acceleration.

**Key Tips:**

-

Use Sylvan Safekeeper and Constant Mists to repeatedly sacrifice lands for protection while generating tokens with Titania or triggering The Gitrog Monster card draw.

-

Crop Rotation can fetch Talon Gates of Madara at instant speed to phase out a key attacker or save your own creature from removal.

-

Bestow Springheart Nantuko onto utility creatures or landfall engines to clone them whenever an additional land enters.

## Weaknesses:

**Critical**

- Symmetrical graveyard hate like Rest in Peace or Dauthi Voidwalker disables the deck's primary recursion engines, Splendid Reclamation lines, and dredge value.

**Moderate**

- Stax pieces that restrict multiple land plays per turn, such as Confounding Conundrum, heavily delay explosive landfall triggers and mana generation.

**Minor**

- Targeted creature removal on key landfall enablers can slow down token generation, though the deck packs strong reanimation and protection spells to recover.

## Key Cards:

-

**Life from the Loam:** Provides continuous land recursion and self-mill to fuel graveyard and landfall engines.

-

**Splendid Reclamation:** Mass recurs all lands from the graveyard to generate massive landfall triggers in a single turn.

-

**Scapeshift:** Sacrifices all lands to tutor key utility lands and trigger massive landfall payoffs simultaneously.

-

**Field of the Dead:** Primary land-based win condition that produces an army of 2/2 Zombie tokens.

-

**Titania, Protector of Argoth:** Converts land sacrifice triggers from fetch lands and engines into lethal 5/3 Elemental tokens.

-

**The Gitrog Monster:** Extra land play enabler and massive draw engine whenever lands enter the graveyard.

-

**Craterhoof Behemoth:** Primary combat finisher to grant massive power and trample to wide token boards.

-

**Lumra, Bellow of the Woods:** Mills cards, brings back all lands from the graveyard, and presents a massive lethal attacker.

-

**Aftermath Analyst:** Early self-mill creature that can be sacrificed later to mass-reanimate all lands from the graveyard.

## Attribute Ratings:

**Consistency: 7/10**

- Extensive land-based tutor packages, heavy self-mill redundancy, and numerous card advantage engines keep land drops and key graveyard pieces readily available.

**Resilience: 8/10**

- Redundant mass-reanimation effects like Splendid Reclamation, Lumra, and Eerie Ultimatum allow the deck to effortlessly rebuild from sweepers and removal.

**Interaction: 6/10**

- The deck runs diverse instant-speed removal like Swords to Plowshares, Assassin's Trophy, and Boseiju, alongside defensive staples like Teferi's Protection and Constant Mists.

**Speed: 5.5/10**

- The deck operates at a solid midrange pace, establishing setup through ramp and self-mill before executing a mass landfall turn. Turn 1 plays an exploration or dork, Turn 2 casts Nature's Lore or self-mill, and Turn 3 deploys Thalia and The Gitrog Monster. By Turn 6 or 7, resolving Scapeshift or Splendid Reclamation with Field of the Dead or Titania floods the board, setting up an immediate Akroma's Will or follow-up Craterhoof Behemoth swing for lethal across all opponents. The bottleneck is safely filling the graveyard with 6 or more lands while assembling a payoff permanent.
