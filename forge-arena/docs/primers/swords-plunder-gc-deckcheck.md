# Swords & Plunder — Game Changers · Strategy Primer
### Rev, Tithe Extractor · mono-black · **Bracket 4 (Optimized)**

*This is the optimized build of Swords & Plunder: the same mono-black evasive
aggro-combo — Treasures, theft, sacrifice, big-mana drain — but faster, more
consistent, and higher-ceiling thanks to 8 Game Changers (fast mana, premium
tutors, card advantage). You still win by connecting with a hard-to-block
creature, snowballing the resources that connect generates, and converting the
lead into a **drain kill** or an **evasive-voltron** swing — but now you get
there several turns sooner and dig for the pieces. The skill ceiling shifts to
**life management**: this deck pays life on many axes, so sequencing lifegain
against your own costs is what separates a win from decking yourself.*

## The commander

**Rev, Tithe Extractor** — {3}{B}, the whole deck is her support structure.
- **On attack:** target creature gains **deathtouch** until end of turn — a
  combat lever to force damage through or trade up. Not a wincon; deploy it to
  push a connect, not for its own sake.
- **On combat damage to a player:** create a **Treasure**, then exile the top of
  *that player's* library face down — **you may cast it**. Triggers **once per
  player hit**, batched across all your creatures, so a single unblockable body
  that hits all three opponents is three Treasures and three stolen cards.
  Evasion + going wide across the pod is the maximizer.

**Read this first:** Rev is a value/tempo engine and the deck is an aggro-combo.
Protect Rev, keep a body connecting, don't over-commit into a wrath, and **watch
your life total** — it is a resource here, not a safety margin.

## What the Game Changers added (play these to full effect)

- **Fast mana — Ancient Tomb, Mox Diamond, Chrome Mox** (joining Sol Ring, Dark
  Ritual, Jet Medallion). These are for *explosive starts* that deploy Rev + a
  connector or ramp straight into the Coffers/drain plan. Note the life cost on
  Ancient Tomb — it's part of the deck's life-tension budget.
- **Premium tutors — Demonic Tutor** (joining Beseech the Mirror, Insatiable
  Avarice). Points at **Cabal Coffers**, **Bloodletter of Aclazotz**, a protection
  piece, or whatever the board demands. With this much tutoring, treat the deck as
  a toolbox — find the answer, don't hope to draw it.
- **Card advantage — Necropotence + The One Ring.** Your refuel engines.
  Necropotence trades life for cards (respect your life total); The One Ring buys a
  **protection turn for Rev** on entry, then draws an escalating pile. Reliquary
  Tower is kept precisely so these don't force discards.
- **Tergrid, God of Fright** — a **theft + edict payoff**. Every time your Grave
  Pact / Dictate of Erebos / Braids forces an opponent to sacrifice — or a
  discard effect hits them — **you put that permanent onto the battlefield under
  your control.** With the edict package running, Tergrid turns the table's board
  into yours. Menace body, too.
- **Orcish Bowmasters** — flash value + a repeatable ping. Its ETB deals 1 to any
  target (kills mana dorks, hatebears, Norin-style x/1s — your former Viridian
  Longbow job), punishes opposing draw engines, and amasses an Orc army. Hold it
  up as instant-speed interaction.

## Subthemes (the play patterns to internalize)

**1. Evasion (the enabler — still the spine).** Nearly every creature is built to
connect: **Dauthi Voidwalker / Nether Traitor / Vashta Nerada** (shadow —
effectively unblockable), **Vault Skirge / Bitterblossom / Bitterbloom Bearer /
Deep-Cavern Bat / Oona's Blackguard** (flying), **Vampire Cutthroat** (skulk), a
menace suite (**Blackbloom Rogue, Snarling Gorehound**), plus grant-evasion glue:
**Shadow Alley Denizen** (black-creature ETB grants intimidate) and **Shizo,
Death's Storehouse** (fear onto Rev herself). *Note vs. the base list:* you cut
Changeling Outcast, so **shadow (Dauthi/Nether Traitor) is now your most reliable
"always connects" line** — lead on it to guarantee the Rev trigger.

**2. Treasure / wealth.** Rev makes Treasures on connect; **Grim Hireling** doubles
it, **Academy Manufactor** turns each into Treasure+Clue+Food, **Sword of Wealth
and Power** and **Warren Soultrader** add more, **Treasure Vault** manufactures
them. Treasures aren't just ramp — they power **Cranial Plating**, fuel **Cabal
Coffers** turns, feed **Inspiring Statuary**'s improvise for a big non-artifact
spell, and bankroll **Beseech the Mirror**'s bargain.

**3. Theft.** A committed subtheme: Rev steals the top of each hit player's
library; **Tinybones** and **Nashi** cast from opponents' graveyards/decks;
**Dauthi Voidwalker** exiles their yard and casts one free; **Deep-Cavern Bat**
strips their hand; **Thieving Varmint** makes mana specifically to cast spells you
don't own; and **Tergrid** steals everything they sacrifice or discard. When you
connect, actually spend the stolen cards — out-gridning a 3-player table is the plan.

**4. Sacrifice / aristocrats.** **Grave Pact** and flash-speed **Dictate of
Erebos** turn any death into a table-wide edict — and each edict feeds **Tergrid**.
Fuel them with token makers (**Bitterblossom, Bitterbloom Bearer, Jadar**) and free
sac outlets (**Warren Soultrader, Phyrexian Tower, Braids, Arisen Nightmare**).
**Nadier's Nightblade** drains 1 per token that leaves — with Bitterblossom + an
outlet that's a per-turn clock. **Morbid Opportunist / Mari** convert the churn to
cards; **Skullclamp** on a 1-toughness token is two cards a pop.

**5. Reanimation.** **Necromancy** (flash), **Chthonian Nightmare** (energy loop),
**Agadeem's Awakening** (mass, scalable), and **Finale of Eternity** (X≥10 returns
your yard) recur your best bodies — usually **Gray Merchant** for a repeatable
drain, or a threat after a wipe. **Yawgmoth's Will** replays your whole graveyard
(rituals, tutors, drains) and is frequently the setup turn for a kill.

**6. Big-mana drain (the primary kill).** **Cabal Coffers + Urborg** (every land a
Swamp) taps for enormous black; **Nykthos** and **K'rrik** (pay life instead of
{B}) multiply it. Dump it into **Exsanguinate**, **Gray Merchant** (mono-black =
huge devotion), or **Finale of Eternity** to drain the table. The fast mana lets
you reach this a turn or two earlier than the base build.

## Bloodletter of Aclazotz — the deck's combo axis

**Bloodletter of Aclazotz** ({1}{B}{B}{B}, 2/4 flyer) is a **force multiplier that
doubles every point of life your opponents lose on your turn.** It turns the deck's
existing drains into kills: **Gray Merchant / Exsanguinate** hit for twice as much
(and refund twice the life), your **combat damage** doubles (Hatred or a Cranial /
Sword swing on an unblockable body can one-shot), and it adds 3 to devotion for a
bigger Gray Merchant. Land it *before* the drain, not after.

It's also a genuine **2-card combo enabler** — pair it with any "target opponent
loses half/N life" effect for a kill (see `advisory-combos.json`: **Peer into the
Abyss, Fraying Omnipotence, Blood Tribute, Torgaar, Rush of Dread** are all one
tutor away). If you draw a halver, Bloodletter converts it to lethal.

## Signature synergy lines

- **K'rrik + ritual → doubled Exsanguinate / Gray Merchant** (with Bloodletter):
  the deck's most explosive kill, often from a near-empty board. Watch your life —
  K'rrik and the pay-life cards make *you* the clock on yourself.
- **Bitterblossom + Skullclamp** → clamp a Faerie for 2 cards a turn; add
  **Nadier's Nightblade** to drain and **Grave Pact** to edict → feeds **Tergrid**.
- **Tergrid + Grave Pact / Dictate / Braids** → every forced sacrifice becomes your
  permanent. Prioritize an edict engine while Tergrid is out.
- **Cranial Plating + an unblockable body** → with a few artifacts/Treasures out
  (and this build runs *more* artifacts), a lethal unblockable swing that also fires
  Rev.
- **Warren Soultrader + Academy Manufactor** → Treasure/Clue/Food churn into mana +
  edicts (Cauldron Familiar is the one piece that closes the loop).
- **Yawgmoth's Will** → replay rituals + tutors + drains for an often-lethal turn.

## Mulligan

Keep hands that (a) deploy an early evasive body or Rev-enabler, or (b) ramp fast
(**Sol Ring, Ancient Tomb, Dark Ritual, Mox Diamond/Chrome Mox, K'rrik**) toward
the Coffers/drain plan — with 2–3 lands or fast mana standing in. A **tutor**
(Demonic/Vampiric-style) plus mana is a keep — you can go find the plan.
**Bitterblossom** and **Necropotence** are premium standalone engines. Ship
no-pressure all-top-end hands: this deck wants to be doing something by turn 2–3,
faster now.

## Threat assessment & life management

- **Protect Rev and your connector:** **Lightning Greaves / Swiftfoot Boots**,
  **Malakir Rebirth**, **Imp's Mischief** (redirect removal/counter off Rev — or
  onto a rival's combo), **Deadly Rollick** (free while you have a commander — hold
  for the scariest threat).
- **Life is a budget, not a buffer.** You pay on many axes — **K'rrik, Necropotence,
  Ancient Tomb, Hatred, Bitterblossom, Bloodletter (phyrexian-adjacent), Toxic
  Deluge**. Sequence lifegain (**Vault Skirge, Barrowgoyf, Vampire Cutthroat,
  Deep-Cavern Bat, Gray Merchant, doubled Exsanguinate**) so you never combo
  yourself out. When in doubt, take the safe line — the deck out-grinds; it doesn't
  need to race its own life total.
- **Sweepers** (**Toxic Deluge, Finale of Eternity**) hit your board too — use them
  from behind and rebuild via reanimation. **Dauthi Voidwalker** shuts off opposing
  recursion; **Sheoldred**'s ETB edicts and flips to a mass-reanimation Saga;
  **Braids** pressures durdle decks.

## Closing — win conditions, ranked

1. **Big-mana drain, doubled:** Coffers/Urborg/K'rrik → Exsanguinate / Gray
   Merchant / Finale, with **Bloodletter** turning "a lot" into "lethal." The most
   reliable kill; often from a near-empty board.
2. **Evasive voltron:** shadow/flying body + **Cranial Plating / Sword / Andúril /
   Hatred** + Rev's deathtouch — lethal beats plus engine value, combat damage
   doubled under Bloodletter.
3. **Aristocrats grind:** Bitterblossom + Nadier + Skullclamp/Grave Pact + Tergrid
   for incremental drain, card advantage, and a stolen board, then a drain to close.
4. **2-card Bloodletter kill:** if you tutor up a life-halver, that's the game.

**Turn shape:** fast mana + evasive body + Rev early → connect for Treasures/theft →
tutor/ramp into Coffers/K'rrik while protecting Rev and banking life → pressure the
pod with Grave Pact/Dictate/Tergrid → close with a doubled drain or a voltron swing.
Hold your free interaction (Deadly Rollick, Imp's Mischief) for the moment that
decides the game.
