# Green White Cushion Castle 25 — DeckCheck analysis

- **Commander:** Sythis, Harvest's Hand
- **Bracket:** 4
- **CRISPI:** consistency 7.0 · interaction 4.25 · resilience 7.25 · speed 4.5 · performance 5.75

## Overview:

Sythis leads a GW enchantress value deck that wins by burying opponents in card advantage and mana. You cast cheap enchantments to draw with Sythis, Argothian Enchantress and Eidolon of Blossoms, ramp with Serra's Sanctum, Gaea's Cradle, Sanctum Weaver and land auras, and protect the board with Privileged Position, Sterling Grove and Sphere of Safety. The main win is infinite mana from Sanctum Weaver + Gauntlets of Light into Luminarch Ascension for infinite 4/4 Angels or Quarantine Field to exile all opposing nonlands, with Concordant Crossroads giving haste. Backup wins are Test of Endurance, large Katilda or Strength of the Harvest attacks, and Earthcraft loops. Sythis is the draw engine, not a combo piece.

## Primer:

**Core Strategy:**

-

Develop draw engine: cast Sythis on turn 2, then chain 1-mana auras like Wild Growth, Utopia Sprawl and Fertile Ground to ramp and trigger Sythis, Argothian Enchantress, Eidolon of Blossoms and Enchantress's Presence.

-

Assemble infinite mana: find Sanctum Weaver + Gauntlets of Light with Enlightened Tutor, Idyllic Tutor, Sterling Grove or Urza's Saga; with 4+ enchantments Weaver taps for 4+, pays {2}{W} to untap, nets infinite mana.

-

Convert to win: use infinite mana in Luminarch Ascension to make infinite 4/4 Angels, or Quarantine Field with X=10+ to exile all opposing nonlands, then give haste with Concordant Crossroads and attack; Test of Endurance and Katilda, Dawnhart Martyr provide alternate kills.

-

Protect and rebuild: keep Sterling Grove, Privileged Position, Destiny Spinner and Sphere of Safety in play, hold Heroic Intervention, Teferi's Protection and Clever Concealment for wipes, and recover with Replenish, Hall of Heliod's Generosity and Shigeki, Jukai Visionary.

**Mulligan Priorities:**

-

**Keep:** 2-3 lands including a Forest for Utopia Sprawl/Wild Growth, Sythis or another enchantress (Argothian Enchantress, Enchantress's Presence), and a cheap enchantment or tutor; keep Serra's Sanctum or Sanctum Weaver if you have early draw.

-

**Avoid:** Hands with 0-1 lands, 4+ lands with no enchantress, only 4+ mana enchantments like Mirari's Wake or Nyxbloom Ancient, or multiple tapped lands (Temple Garden, Canopy Vista, Valgavoth's Lair) with no turn 1-2 play.

**Key Tips:**

-

Sequence auras before creatures: play Wild Growth/Utopia Sprawl first to trigger Sythis draw and grow Sanctum Weaver's mana count toward the 4-enchantment threshold for Gauntlets loop.

-

Hold Destiny Spinner and Sterling Grove to make enchantments uncounterable and give shroud before committing Luminarch Ascension or Gauntlets; use Weaver of Harmony to copy Ascension or Sanctum Weaver triggers.

-

Manage Luminarch Ascension counters early: avoid losing life on opponents' turns so you get a quest counter each end step; you need 4 counters before infinite mana matters.

-

Use Aura Shards and Earthcraft carefully: Aura Shards destroys opposing stax on each creature entry, and Earthcraft with basic lands plus Mirari's Wake/Nyxbloom Ancient can generate infinite mana and tokens even without Gauntlets.

## Weaknesses:

**Critical**

- Mass enchantment removal (Farewell, Bane of Progress, Back to Nature, Tranquility) - 84% of nonland cards are enchantments or depend on enchantments, including draw, ramp and both combo lines; only Heroic Intervention, Teferi's Protection and Clever Concealment answer at instant speed, so an unanswered wipe costs 2+ turns to rebuild even with Replenish.

**Moderate**

-

Graveyard hate (Rest in Peace, Grafdigger's Cage, Leyline of the Void) shuts off Replenish, Redress Fate, Hall of Heliod's Generosity, Shigeki and Katilda disturb, removing the main rebuild plan.

-

Rule-of-Law effects (Deafening Silence, Rule of Law, Curse of Exhaustion) slow the 1-enchantment-per-turn chain; deck can answer them with Aura Shards, Banishing Light, Grasp of Fate and Quarantine Field but must draw the answer at sorcery speed.

-

Almost no stack interaction - 0 counterspells and only 4 instant-speed pieces - so an opponent's counterspell on Idyllic Tutor, Gauntlets of Light or Luminarch Ascension, or instant-speed exile of Sanctum Weaver in response to Gauntlets equip, stops the win with no free protection.

**Minor**

-

Single-target removal on Sanctum Weaver or Nyxbloom Ancient before you have protection delays infinite mana; Gift of Immortality and Asceticism help but cost mana to set up.

-

Low land count (32) with 4 always-tapped and 6 conditional-tapped lands causes missed land drops and slows Sythis curve; mulligans are punished.

-

Sphere of Safety and Privileged Position do not stop exile wipes or sacrifice effects.

## Key Cards:

-

**Sanctum Weaver:** Main mana engine; taps for X where X is enchantment count, core of Gauntlets of Light infinite mana.

-

**Gauntlets of Light:** Untap outlet for Weaver; with 4+ enchantments nets infinite mana, also enables toughness-based combat.

-

**Luminarch Ascension:** Primary outlet for infinite mana; with 4 quest counters makes infinite 4/4 Angels for combat win.

-

**Nyxbloom Ancient:** Triples mana from lands and Weaver, accelerates to 7+ mana combos and makes Earthcraft loops infinite.

-

**Enchantress's Presence:** Redundant draw engine with Sythis and Argothian Enchantress to chain enchantments.

-

**Sterling Grove:** Gives other enchantments shroud and tutors for Gauntlets, Ascension or Sphere of Safety.

-

**Serra's Sanctum:** Explosive white mana equal to enchantments, funds combo and Quarantine Field for X.

-

**Earthcraft:** Untaps basic lands with creatures; with land auras, Mirari's Wake and Nyxbloom creates infinite mana/tokens.

-

**Replenish:** Mass recursion that rebuilds entire board after wipe, key to resilience.

-

**Aura Shards:** Main repeatable removal for artifacts/enchantments on creature entry, answers stax pieces.

## Attribute Ratings:

**Consistency: 7/10**

- Tutors are the binding factor with 6 reliable finders (Enlightened Tutor, Idyllic Tutor, Sterling Grove, Urza's Saga, Moon-Blessed Cleric, Nature's Rhythm) but only one premium instant tutor, while draw is strong from Sythis in the command zone plus Argothian Enchantress, Eidolon of Blossoms and Enchantress's Presence; 32 lands is 7 below the target for this curve, so you mulligan more often despite good card flow.

**Resilience: 7.25/10**

- Combo channel is binding with 2.25 lines and 6 realizable combos, supported by 15 protection pieces including 3 mass protection spells (Heroic Intervention, Teferi's Protection, Clever Concealment) and strong recursion (Replenish, Hall of Heliod's Generosity, Gift of Immortality) plus 6 rebuild engines, but 84% enchantment exposure applies a penalty because one mass enchantment wipe hits draw, ramp and win conditions together.

**Interaction: 4.25/10**

- Stack/timing is binding with only 4 instant-speed pieces and 0 counterspells or free interaction among 27 total pieces; you answer creatures, artifacts and enchantments well at sorcery speed with Aura Shards, Banishing Light, Grasp of Fate and Quarantine Field, but you rarely stop wins on the stack.

**Speed: 4.5/10**

- Slow-midrange enchantress. Median goldfish is turn 7-8: T2 Sythis, T3 Enchantress's Presence + Utopia Sprawl/Wild Growth, T4 Sanctum Weaver, T5 Gauntlets of Light for infinite mana once you have 4 enchantments (Weaver taps for 4, pays 3 to untap), T6-7 Luminarch Ascension reaches 4 quest counters then makes infinite 4/4 Angels with haste from Concordant Crossroads. Bottleneck is needing 4 enchantments for net mana, 4 opponent end steps for Ascension, and sorcery-speed tutors to assemble the two-card combo.
