# Colorless cEDH 23 — DeckCheck analysis

- **Commander:** Liberator, Urza's Battlethopter
- **Bracket:** 4
- **CRISPI:** consistency 8.0 · interaction 5.25 · resilience 9.0 · speed 5.5 · performance 6.94

## Overview:

This colorless artifact prison deck uses Liberator, Urza's Battlethopter to flash in stax and combo pieces at instant speed. Its primary plan is to lock the board with Sphere of Resistance, Trinisphere, Lodestone Golem, Grafdigger's Cage and The Tabernacle at Pendrell Vale, then assemble infinite colorless mana with Rings of Brighthearth, Basalt Monolith, Grim Monolith, Voltaic Key, Manifold Key, Clock of Omens and Forsaken Monument. Infinite mana wins through Walking Ballista or Staff of Domination, with Aetherflux Reservoir as backup. Secondary paths use Eldrazi and planeswalkers for value and removal. Karn, the Great Creator acts as the hidden commander, tutoring artifacts and shutting off opponent artifacts.

## Primer:

**Core Strategy:**

-

Establish prison: deploy fast mana (Sol Ring, Mana Vault, Grim Monolith, Ancient Tomb, Mishra's Workshop) then flash in stax at instant speed with Liberator - Sphere of Resistance, Trinisphere, Lodestone Golem, God-Pharaoh's Statue, Grafdigger's Cage, Torpor Orb.

-

Assemble infinite colorless mana: Rings of Brighthearth + Basalt Monolith/Grim Monolith + Voltaic Key/Manifold Key/Clock of Omens + Forsaken Monument, or Deserted Temple + Scorched Ruins + Rings, or Metalworker + Rings + Monoliths.

-

Convert infinite mana to win: Walking Ballista for 40 damage, Staff of Domination to draw the deck and loop, or Aetherflux Reservoir with infinite casts. Use Liberator flash to combo on an opponent's end step.

-

Grind without combo: Conduit of Ruin and Sanctum of Ugin find Kozilek or Emrakul, Ugin the Spirit Dragon and Ugin the Ineffable control colored permanents, Karn and Mystic Forge provide card advantage.

**Mulligan Priorities:**

-

**Keep:** 2-3 lands including a fast land (Ancient Tomb, Mishra's Workshop, Crystal Vein) plus 1-2 fast mana and a tutor or draw engine (Expedition Map, Moonsilver Key, Urza's Saga, Inventors' Fair, Sensei's Divining Top, Mystic Forge). A hand with Rings, Monolith, or Forsaken Monument plus ramp is keepable.

-

**Avoid:** Hands with no ramp, one land with no acceleration, or only high-cost Eldrazi and planeswalkers (Emrakul, Kozilek, Ugin) with no way to cast them. Hands with only stax and no mana or card flow.

**Key Tips:**

-

Play Liberator with flash to hold up interaction. You can flash in Rings, Stax, or the infinite mana pieces on opponent end steps and untap to win, and Liberator grows when you cast spells larger than its power.

-

Sequence cost reducers first: Ugin the Ineffable and Foundry Inspector make Mystic Forge + Sensei's Divining Top loops cheaper and reduce the 7-mana combo cost. Forsaken Monument doubles every {C} tap and must be protected.

-

Tutor correctly: Scrapyard Recombiner and Kuldotha Forgemaster sacrifice artifacts to put combo pieces directly into play, Inventors' Fair and Urza's Saga find 0-1 mana artifacts, Planar Bridge is slow at 8 mana - use it only with infinite mana.

-

Protect the outlet: do not expose Walking Ballista until you have infinite mana. Staff of Domination can draw into Ballista and also gains life and untaps creatures, giving you a second outlet if Ballista is exiled.

## Weaknesses:

**Critical**

-

80% artifact dependence - Collector Ouphe, Null Rod, Stony Silence and Vandalblast shut off most mana, tutors, and combo pieces. Deck has few answers to colorless artifacts (All Is Dust and Ugin only hit colored permanents) and must rely on Karn, the Great Creator, Blast Zone, or Spatial Contortion.

-

Single main finisher dependence - Walking Ballista is the only efficient infinite-mana outlet; if exiled and Staff of Domination/Aetherflux Reservoir are not found, infinite mana does not win. All win lines share Rings/Monolith/Key artifacts.

**Moderate**

-

Limited stack interaction - only 3 counterspells (Kozilek the Great Distortion, Warping Wail, Chalice of the Void) and 2 instant-speed answers, no free interaction or turn protection, so a counter on Rings or Ballista can cost 2 turns.

-

Land-light at 29 lands - misses land drops and struggles to recover from Strip Mine/Wasteland or Scorched Ruins sacrifice. Own Sphere of Resistance and Trinisphere also tax your own combo turn.

**Minor**

-

Self-tax from The Tabernacle at Pendrell Vale and life loss from Ancient Tomb - pressures your own creatures and life total under Aetherflux Reservoir plan.

-

Grafdigger's Cage and Weathered Runestone are symmetric and block your own recursion (Codex Shredder, Buried Ruin, Crucible of Worlds) if left unchecked.

## Attribute Ratings:

**Consistency: 8/10**

- High tutor density (Conduit of Ruin, Sanctum of Ugin, Scrapyard Recombiner, Kuldotha Forgemaster, Inventors' Fair, Moonsilver Key, Urza's Saga, Planar Bridge) and 15+ draw engines (Memory Jar, Kozilek, Ugin, War Room, Sword of Fire and Ice) give strong finding power, but 29 lands is well below the ~39 needed for colorless, creating missed land drops and mulligan pressure that caps consistency.

**Resilience: 9/10**

- 15 realizable combos across 3 distinct lines with many redundant threats and 15 stax pieces provide multiple angles, plus 3 protection pieces (Sword of Fire and Ice, Sword of Forge and Frontier, The One Ring) and 6+ recursion pieces (Karn, Ugin the Ineffable, Codex Shredder, Buried Ruin, Crucible of Worlds) to rebuild, but 80% of the nonland deck is artifact-typed or artifact-dependent so artifact hate applies a clear penalty.

**Interaction: 5.25/10**

- 43 interaction pieces look broad on board (strong vs artifacts, creatures, planeswalkers and graveyards) but stack interaction is thin with only 3 counterspells and 2 instant-speed answers, no free interaction and no turn protection, so the deck interacts mainly at sorcery speed through Ugin, All Is Dust, and stax.

**Speed: 5.5/10**

- Moderately slow stax-combo. Median goldfish: T1-2 Sol Ring/Grim Monolith/Ancient Tomb into Thran Dynamo, T3-4 Rings of Brighthearth + Voltaic Key/Manifold Key + Forsaken Monument, T5 Basalt Monolith/Grim Monolith for infinite colorless, T6 Walking Ballista for 40 or Staff of Domination to draw into Ballista. With 29 lands and 8 tutors for a 3-piece 7-mana combo plus a single main finisher, the nut T4 is rare and you reliably need an extra turn to find the outlet, anchoring the median at turns 6-7.
