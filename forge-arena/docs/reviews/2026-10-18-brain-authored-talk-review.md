## P0 — would break play, the voice, or the ledger

### F1. The stance hooks' `continue` skips bookkeeping the ledger, kill attribution and loop detection depend on
**Plan (§5):** "hooks, one line each at the top of the existing branches in `scan_events`, before the dice line … the dice line for that seat and event is skipped (`continue`)".
**Code:** the branches do bookkeeping before and around the dice line:
- `damage` — `events.py:928-929` sets `_last_hit_by` (read at elimination for the **kill line**, `events.py:1212-1222`); `events.py:931-933` counts `_hits_from` (the grudge memory). A top-of-branch `continue` loses both.
- `attack` — `events.py:900` `note_attack` (BL-63: a small-talk truce is not struck across a declared attack); `events.py:903-907` `_deal_broken` → `break_deal` writes the **deals ledger** `broken` record and the notes to both brains (`scheduler.py:1010-1028`). Skipped = a betrayal never recorded, the wronged brain never told.
- `cast` by another seat — `events.py:967, 994` `_gc_cast_seen.add` + `raise_threat` (the gc crows again at its next cast; the threat pin stops following game changers); `events.py:963, 990` `loop_tick`; `events.py:985-986` the turn-summary cast count ("land, go" after a cast); `events.py:1006-1014` the flurry clock; `events.py:1018-1019` `cast_tick` (LOOP_CASTS).
- `left` — `events.py:1027-1031` `loop_tick` for a self-bounced gc; `events.py:1039` `_sweeps`; the sweeper's/remover's own line and the sweep atom.
- `countered` — `events.py:1065-1067` `_countered_n`.
The one existing `continue` after `grudge` (`events.py:937`) is *below* all the damage bookkeeping, which is exactly why it is safe; the plan's hooks are above it.
**Revision:** never `continue` an event for a stance. A stance is a `_bark` substituted at the exact site of **that seat's own** dice line (see F2 for which sites exist), with the event's ctx; all bookkeeping runs as today.

### F2. "A stance replaces today's dice line for that seat" is false for five of the eight triggers — today's line belongs to the actor, and the victim's reaction is a chain reply
**Plan (§5 table):** on_attacked replaces brace/why-me/no-blocks; on_gc "the bystander chain reaction"; on_commander oh-no/brace/read-that; on_big_spell wow/nice-play/read-that; on_removal oh-no; on_counter got-countered/again-countered.
**Code:** for an AI actor the event line is the actor's — `big-swing`/`poke`/`attack-you` by the attacker (`events.py:908-916`), `game-changer`/`commander-cast`/narration by the caster (`events.py:992-998`), `removal` by the remover (`events.py:1056`), `sweep` by the sweeper (`events.py:1042`), `counter` by the counterer (`events.py:1073-1075`). The defender's `brace`/`why-me`, the owner's `oh-no`/`scoff`, the bystander's `gc-react`/`oh-no`/`kill-that`, the victim's `scoff` after a counter are **chain replies** planned in `after_spoken` → `plan_reply` from `chains.json` (`big-swing`: open→no-blocks, target→brace/why-me/attack-them; `removal`: target→oh-no/scoff/you-suck; `game-changer`: bystander→gc-react/oh-no/kill-that; `commander-cast`: bystander→oh-no/brace/read-that; `cast-big`: bystander→wow/oh-no/read-that/sure; `counter`: target→scoff). Only the **human-actor** sub-branches speak the reactor's line directly (`events.py:917-924`, `955-983`, `1057-1060`, `1076-1079`), and only `on_hit` (`events.py:935-945`) and `on_sweep` (`events.py:1044-1047`) are the stance-holder's own line in both cases. The victim's `got-countered` never fires at all when the counterer is a voiced seat (the `elif` chain at `events.py:1068-1079`).
**Consequence:** "replace + continue" silences the actor (and its atom, and the chain the table expects); "add without replacing" gives two lines for one moment plus a possible chain reply of a different id (`said_this_turn` dedupes only the same id, `scheduler.py:679-682`).
**Revision:** two shapes, no new hooks: (a) **own-line substitution** at the six own-line sites above (the dice `pid`/`p` become the stance's id and 1.0; the ctx stays the event's, so `aggressor`, `human_cause`, `card` are right by construction); (b) **pre-chosen reply**: in `after_spoken` before `plan_reply` (`scheduler.py:622-626`), if the spoken opener maps to a trigger (`big-swing|poke|attack-you→on_attacked` for `ctx.targets`; `game-changer|gc-*-cast→on_gc`; `commander-cast|cmd-*-cast→on_commander`; `cast-big→on_big_spell`; `removal→on_removal` for `ctx.targets`; `sweep→on_sweep`; `counter→on_counter` for `ctx.targets`) and a target/bystander seat holds that stance, enqueue it as the hop-1 reply with p=1.0 through the same link/gap/prio/ctx a planned reply gets (`scheduler.py:639-653`) and skip `plan_reply`. That inherits A14, the seat guard, `said_this_turn`, the hop priority and "a new turn ends the exchange" for free. Honest limitation: (b) fires 0.25 s after the actor's line and only if that line played; say so in the plan instead of "performed at once".
