# EDH rules card (seat context)

- Commander / EDH: 4 players, 40 life each, singleton 99 + commander.
- Commander damage: 21 combat damage from one commander eliminates that player.
- Command zone: your commander starts there; recasts cost {2} more each time
  it has returned there. Color identity restricts your deck's mana symbols.
- Priority basics: sorcery-speed plays only in your own main phases with an
  empty stack; instants/flash any time you have priority. The stack resolves
  top-down; each resolution passes priority again.
- Combat: attackers tap unless vigilance; blocks are declared per-attacker;
  trample assigns excess through blockers; first strike/deathtouch/lifelink
  apply as printed. Summoning sickness stops attacking/tap abilities, not blocks.
- Multiplayer reality: threat = mana open + cards in hand + board trajectory,
  not just life totals. The archenemy draws the table's removal; overextending
  into open mana invites blowouts; politics are damage math over time.
